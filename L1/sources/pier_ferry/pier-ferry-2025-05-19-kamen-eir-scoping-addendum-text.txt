Is the project being honestly represented to the public?

The title of “Pier-Ferry Project” implies that reconstructing 500 feet of
recreational pier is a major goal of the project, with something approaching
parity in terms of the project’s priorities.

After extensive analysis, the City’s parking consultants determined that 389
parking spaces for ferry passengers will be required, and goes to some
lengths to explain how these additional parked cars will not significantly
affect recreational access to the waterfront. How many parking spaces will
be required to serve recreational pier visitors? ZERO, according to the
Kittelson parking studies. See the problem here?

Also, the “E” for “Emergency” reduces the credibility of the WETA meme,
especially when considered along with electrification. A true emergency
ferry system would use vehicle-carrying ferries, capable of transporting
ambulances and fire trucks across the Bay. This could save lives in the first
few hours after a disaster event. Passenger-only ferries address the
inconvenience of being stuck on the wrong side of the Bay, but do little to
protect life and limb.

True emergency ferries need to be agile and versatile enough to load
emergency vehicles from any accessible boat ramp – we don’t know which
terminals or ramps will be cut off due to liquefaction or downed
infrastructure.

And finally, electric ferries for emergencies? The shore power grid is likely
to be interrupted, and electric ferries need to recharge during dwell time at
the terminals. The work-around is fixed diesel-generators and batteries at
the terminals, but that essentially doubles the cost of the prime movers.

From Agenda Item 9, Weta Board meeting: December 9, 2021: “…an all-
electric ferry fleet may be cited as a milestone for incentivizing a community
to come out and support the development of a new ferry terminal.”

Without an enforceable statutory requirement for electric ferries on new
routes, this reads like a disingenuous bait-and-switch promise designed to
calm the opposition.

The scope of the EIR needs to include a rigorous review of WETA’s actual
commitment to electrification at scale. The EIR also needs to evaluate the
efficacy of the ferry system in response to a major earthquake, fire, or
widespread infrastructure failure. Would it be more honest to drop the “E?”
