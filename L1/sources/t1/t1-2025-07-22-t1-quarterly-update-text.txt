                                              T1 Bond, Phase 2 Project Updates
                                              We are using funding from T1 bonds to improve sidewalks, storm drains,
                                              streets, parks, community centers, and other key infrastructure and
                                              facilities.

                                              Measure T1 provides $100 million of funding from bond revenue to pay for
                                              improvements to City infrastructure and facilities. Phase 1 projects are
                                              complete. This is an update on Phase 2 projects.

                                       Willard Park Clubhouse Replacement Project
                                       A ribbon-cutting ceremony took place on June 25th to celebrate the opening
                                       of the new Willard Clubhouse; a space designed with the community in
                                       mind. The clubhouse includes versatile community rooms, a kitchen, staff
                                       office, and gender-neutral restrooms. Sustainability was a key focus in its
                                       design, featuring eco-friendly elements such as solar panels, battery
                                       storage, radiant floor heating, and bird-safe glass. A new public restroom is
                                       also available nearby, conveniently located next to the tennis courts.

                                     MLK Jr. Youth Services Y.A.P Renovation Project




                                     MLK JR. Youth Service Y.A.P. Community Center Rendering


                                     The MLK Jr. Youth Services Center is closed for construction until spring 2027.
                                     During this time, the Young Adult Project (YAP) office is operating out of
                                     the Frances Albrier Community Center.

                                     The City is upgrading the MLK Jr. Youth Services Center to make the 1950s
                                     building safer, more modern, and easier for everyone to use. This includes
                                     earthquake safety improvements and other major repairs. For more details,
                                     visit our project page.

                                     T1 Phase 2 Project Updates
                                     Facilities/Buildings

                                          African American Holistic Resource Center Project – ZAB approval
                                          scheduled for September/October. After approval, staff and consultants
                                          will be working on final design.
                                          MLK Jr. Youth Services Center – Construction started July 2025.
                                          Willard Park Clubhouse – The Willard Park Clubhouse held a grand
                                          opening event on June 25, 2025, and will have an open house on August
                                          5, 2025. Full programming will begin when we receive permanent
                                          occupancy.
                                          South Berkeley Senior Center – In design phase, public process
                                          beginning int the Fall of 2025.
                                          1947 Center Street Window Replacement – Landmarks Preservation
                                          Commission process is complete. The next step is to submit the project
                                          for permitting.
                                          Fire Station #2 – This project is currently in pre-construction. Notice to
                                          Proceed will be issued shortly.
                                          Fire Station #6 – This project is currently in permitting
                                          PW Corporation Yard Improvements:

                                                Green Room & Building H – Received 4 bids and several bid
                                                protests delaying award. Anticipated Council approval of award
                                                following summer recess in September 2025.
                                                Wash Station – Construction starting September 2025.

                                     Restrooms

                                          Ohlone Park Restroom – Construction phase started. Pre-fab restroom
                                          is currently slated to be delivered in August 2025.
                                          Willard Park Restroom – In construction, anticipated completion in
                                          August 2025.
                                          Telegraph/Channing – Project was completed in January 2025 and
                                          grand opening held February 11, 2025.
                                          Adeline/Alcatraz – Finalizing design.
                                          San Pablo/University – Community Meeting #2 held 2/4/25.
                                          Consultant was hired to re-evaluate the restroom location.
                                          Telegraph-Channing Parking Garage Restrooms & Elevators –
                                          Notice to Proceed issued, mobilization in late summer 2025 pending
                                          elevator procurement.
                                          Harrison Park Restroom – In conceptual design.
                                          Cesar Chavez Restroom on Spinnaker – Pursuing an alternate
                                          location on the south side of Spinnaker Way.
                                          K Dock Restroom – Project is expected to bid in late July 2025.
                                          Construction is expected to start in late fall to early winter of 2025.

                                     Parks

                                          John Hinkel Park Playground and Amphitheater Improvements –
                                          Project complete.
                                          King Pool Tile and Plaster Replacement – Project complete.
                                          Grove Park Field Renovation – Projects complete.

                                          completion expected at the end of Summer 2025.

                                          Improvements –Project complete.
