To:             Honorable Mayor and Members of the City
From:           Paul Buddenhagen, City Manager
Re:             Opening of the 24-Hour Telegraph-Channing Public Restroom

On Tuesday Feb. 11 the City of Berkeley will open a new, permanent 24-hour public
restroom on Telegraph Avenue, addressing a key need along one of the densest and
busiest commercial districts in Berkeley. The ADA-accessible and solar-powered
restroom at the intersection of Channing Way will be cleaned multiple times a day and
maintained regularly in partnership with the Telegraph Business Improvement District.
The facility is built to maintain user privacy while also deterring potential illegal
activities with carefully designed sight lines at the top and bottom of the stall.

The location was identified in a 2020 Citywide Restroom Study as the top Citywide
priority for a 24-hour public restroom due to the lack of easily accessible public
restrooms, the street’s heavy pedestrian usage, and complaints of open urination and
defecation.

Following the opening of this restroom, two more public restrooms are planned for
construction: one at Alcatraz Avenue and Adeline Street and another at San Pablo and
University Avenues. Public outreach on those projects is underway, including the
scheduling of virtual community meetings.

The general public is invited to join leaders from the City, UC Berkeley, and TBID to
celebrate the opening.

 Restroom Features and Operations
 The new public restroom is a prefabricated model known for its durability, accessibility,
 and ease of maintenance. Referred to as a “Portland Loo,” this stand-alone structure
 offers several key features designed to enhance the user experience and minimize
 environmental impact:
    •   Hygiene: The restroom includes a handwashing station with running water and
        soap along with a hand sanitizer dispenser.

    •   Accessibility: The Portland Loo restroom is ADA-compliant. Entry to and exit
        from the restroom can be easily done on a wheelchair. Multiple handrails are
        located for easy grabbing for those who need support.

    •   Sustainability: The restroom is powered by solar panels on its rooftop,
        reducing energy consumption and minimizing its carbon footprint.

    •   Security: The restroom is constructed with graffiti-resistant materials and
        limited sight lines at top and bottom to deter illegal activities while maintaining
        absolute user privacy.
    •   Art Wrap: The interior and exterior of the restroom will feature a decorative
        wrap with artwork by Forest Wolf Kell who painted the "Marvelous Miss Mars"
        mural. This new public artwork will complement the existing mural next to the
        restroom and enhance the aesthetic appeal of the facility.

 The City of Berkeley, in partnership with the Telegraph Business Improvement District
 (TBID), will be responsible for the ongoing operation and maintenance of the restroom.
 This will include:

    •   Daily Cleaning: The facility will be cleaned and sanitized multiple times daily to
        ensure a hygienic environment for users.

    •   Regular Maintenance: Routine maintenance will be conducted to address any
        repairs or upkeep needs, ensuring the facility remains in good working order.
    •   Supplies: The restroom will continuously be stocked with essential supplies,
        such as toilet paper and hand soap, to ensure a comfortable and convenient
        user experience.

    •   Additional Support: The Telegraph District Ambassadors will provide additional
        staff presence and community support around the restroom, including handling
        harmful and threatening situations through trained conflict resolution and de-
        escalation skills.

 This collaboration between the City and the TBID has long been critical to the vibrancy
 of the Southside neighborhood. With this partnership, the City can work to deliver long-
 term success of this first-of-a-kind public restroom in Berkeley.

 Restroom Funding and Partnerships
 The Telegraph and Channing Public Restroom project is funded through a combination
 of sources:

       •    Measure T1 Funds: A significant portion of the funding comes from Measure
            T1, a voter-approved measure in 2016 that provides funds for improvements to
            City infrastructure and facilities. This funding source highlights the community's
            commitment to investing in essential public services like this restroom project.

       •    UC Berkeley Settlement: Additional funding is provided through the UC
            Berkeley Settlement, an agreement between the City and UC Berkeley that
            includes provisions for supporting public restroom facilities. This partnership is
            emblematic of the shared commitment of the City and the University to
            addressing community needs.
 The total project budget is approximately $488,000, which covers the unit procurement
 cost of around $160,000 and the construction cost of about $328,000. Additionally,
 $210,000 was budgeted for maintenance for FY2025/2026. The decorative art wrap
 has been sponsored and donated by the Telegraph Business Improvement District with
 support from the UC Berkeley Chancellors Community Partnership Fund.

 We encourage community members to stay informed about the project's progress and
 to share their feedback and suggestions via the Capital Projects page for Restrooms in
 the Public Right of Way. Together, we are working to enhance our public spaces and
 improve the quality of life for everyone in Berkeley.
