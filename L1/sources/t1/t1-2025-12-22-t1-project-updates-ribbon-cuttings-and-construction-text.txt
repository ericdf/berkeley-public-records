              T1 Bond, Phase 2 Project Updates
              We are using funding from T1 bonds to improve sidewalks, storm drains,
              streets, parks, community centers, and other key infrastructure and
              facilities.

              Measure T1 provides $100 million of funding from bond revenue to pay for
              improvements to City infrastructure and facilities. Phase 1 projects are
              complete. This is an update on Phase 2 projects.

D & E Docks Replacement Project
Almost all the in-water construction for the new docks such as piles,
gangway, floating docks and various dock accessories have been
completed. The primary focus to complete the project in Spring, 2026 is
landside utility connections to serve the new electrical and domestic water
systems that require close coordination with PG&E and EBMUD.




D&E Docks at the Berkeley Marina


Corporation Yard Improvements
Construction is currently underway on several projects at the Corporation
Yard, including the Wash Station, Building H, and the Green Room. The
Wash Station improvements involve replacing a section of the sewer line
and installing a new valve to improve water flow, reduce the risk of sewer
backups, and strengthen best management practices. Upgrades to Building
H include a new roof, fresh exterior paint, and asbestos remediation. The
Green Room project features comprehensive structural and architectural
upgrades, including a full interior remodel with new restrooms, flooring, and
roofing, as well as modernized mechanical, electrical, and plumbing
systems.

Restrooms

       Harrison Park Restroom – Conceptual design completed.
       Ohlone Park Restroom – Construction in progress. Pre-fabricated

Waterfront

      Replacement of Docks D & E at the Berkeley Marina – Phase 1 of
       Construction (in-water demolition of docks) was completed in November
       2024. Phase 2 landside and waterside improvements are in
       construction. Waterside improvements such as new dock floats, piles,
       and dock accessories are nearing completion. The major landside
       construction that the work is focusing on is related to the new PG&E
       electrical infrastructure. Construction is anticipated to be completed by
       February 2026.
      K Dock Restroom – Construction contract was awarded by City
       Council on November 18, 2025. Construction is anticipated to start in
       early 2026 with completion around mid 2026.
      Marina Pilings Replacement – Project completed
      Docks F & G Redecking at the Berkeley Marina – The existing
       timber decking has reached the end of its useful life. Most of deck
       boards have dry rotted with splits and they can no longer be fastened to
       the docks securely and must be replaced. Construction started
       December 8, 2025 and is anticipated to be complete, February, 2026.
