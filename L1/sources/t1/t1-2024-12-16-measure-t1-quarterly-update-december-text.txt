                                           T1 Bond, Phase 2 Project Updates
                                           We are using funding from T1 bonds to improve sidewalks, storm drains,
                                           streets, parks, community centers, and other key infrastructure and
                                           facilities.

                                           Measure T1 provides $100 million of funding from bond revenue to pay for
                                           improvements to City infrastructure and facilities. Phase 1 projects are
                                           complete. This is an update on Phase 2 projects.

                                           Get an overview of Phase Two selected projects.

T1 Phase 2 Project Updates
Facilities/Buildings

     African American Holistic Resource Center Project – In conceptual
     design phase, preparing zoning package.
     MLK Jr. Youth Services Center – Plans submitted for building permit
     review. Anticipate bidding in early 2025 and starting construction in
     Summer 2025.
     Willard Park Clubhouse – In construction.
     SBSC – In design phase, preparing for community meetings and
     drawings for building permits in Spring 2025.
     Fire Station No. 2 – Advertised for public bid in December 2024,
     anticipated construction in Spring/Summer 2025 following award and
     contract execution.
     Fire Station No. 6 –Project on hold after funding reallocated. Funding
     returning next year.
     1947 Center Street Improvements –The design complete and the
     structural alteration permit has been submitted to the building
     department for review.
     PW Corporation Yard Improvements:
           Green Room & Building H – Design and permitting phase
           complete. Will be advertised for public bid beginning of 2025.
           Wash Station – Currently in contract execution phase. Anticipated
           construction in the beginning of 2025.

Restrooms

     Telegraph/Channing – PG&E scope completed in October 2024. The
     Contractor has mobilized, and project estimated completion is expected
     by February 2025.
     Adeline/Alcatraz – Currently under PG&E design, with completion
     expected in March 2025.
     San Pablo/University – Design completed. Currently working on
     securing an encroachment permit with Caltrans.
     Telegraph-Channing Parking Garage Restrooms & Elevators –
     Currently in contract execution phase. Anticipated construction in Spring
     2025.
     Harrison Park Restroom – In conceptual design.
     Cesar Chavez Restroom on Spinnaker – Site location directly on
     Spinnaker Way where existing port-a-potty and ADA parking spaces are
     located has been selected. A self-cleaning restroom is being pursued. A
     second community meeting was held on October 30, 2024. Currently the
     project is moving forward from conceptual design to the formal design
     process. Pre-Construction work to bring underground utilities to the
     project site before construction starts. Construction is expected to start
     in 2025.
     Ohlone Park Restroom – Design complete, construction contract
     awarded. Project in pre-construction phase. Construction to commence
     in January 2025 but anticipating a long lead time for pre-fabricated
     restroom, on-site work will not start until Spring 2025.
     Willard Park Restroom – In construction.
     K Dock Restroom – Construction is expected to start in 2025. Plans
     have been developed and the project is in the permitting phase.

Parks

     John Hinkel Park – Playground and Amphitheater Improvements were
     completed and celebrated on July 16, 2022.
     King Pool Tile and Plaster Replacement – Construction completed,
     and pool is open.
     Grove Park Field and Playground Renovation Project– Playground
     renovations were completed and celebrated on March 6, 2024. Field
     renovations were completed and celebrated on May 16, 2024.
     Civic Center Park Upper Plaza Improvements – Design complete,
     construction contract awarded. Project in pre-construction phase.
     Construction at the plaza to commence in January 2025.
     Ohlone Park (East) Playground Replacement and Site
     Improvements –Renovations were completed, and re-opening was
     celebrated on September 23, 2023.
     Ohlone Park Lighting – Design complete, construction contract
     awarded. Project in pre-construction phase. Construction to commence in
     January 2025, but anticipating a long lead time for materials, on-site
     work may not start until Spring 2025.
     Aquatic Park Tide Tubes Clean Out – Soil Removal: Phase 1B project
     is completed.
