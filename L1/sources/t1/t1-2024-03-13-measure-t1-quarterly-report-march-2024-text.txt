T1 BOND, PHASE 2 PROJECT UPDATES
We are using funding from T1 bonds to improve sidewalks, storm drains,
streets, parks, community centers, and other key infrastructure and
facilities. Measure T1 provides $100 million of funding from bond revenue
to pay for improvements to City infrastructure and facilities. Phase 1
projects are complete. This is an update on Phase 2 projects.

OHLONE PARK OPENS (MURAL GARDEN AREA)




Ohlone Park Mural Garden

T1 PHASE 2 PROJECT UPDATES
Facilities/Buildings

   •    African American Holistic Resource Center: Planning and
        programming phase almost complete, scheduled to open July 2026.
   •    MLK Jr. Youth Service Center Renovation: Planning phase
        complete. Design Documentation phase has begun, scheduled to open
        May 2025.
   •    City Building at 1947 Center St – In planning phase. Windows and
        HVAC improvements design anticipated to be complete in February
        2025.
   •    South Berkeley Senior Center – In design phase for structural
        strengthening and other improvements. Waiting on result of recently
        submitted CRC grant.
   •    Fire Station 2 – Completing design documents. Submittal of permit
        approval in October 2024.
   •    Fire Station 6 – (Planning project only) In schematic design phase.
   •    Corporation Yard Improvements:
            • Wash Station – Design phase at 70%.
            • H-Roof Repair – Design and permitting complete.
            • Green Room, Lockers, Bathroom, Training Room – Design and
               permitting complete.

Restrooms

   •    ROW Restrooms at University/San Pablo; Alcatraz/Adeline –
        Bidding, Completion June 2025.
   •    ROW Restrooms at Channing/Telegraph- Bidding complete.
        Construction will begin after PG&E work. Estimate construction
        completion in June of 2025.
   •    Telegraph/Channing Garage Restroom Improvements – Restroom
        project has been combined with elevator replacement project and is
        currently in permitting phase.
   •    Harrison Park Restroom: Conceptual design phase almost complete.
   •    Cesar Chavez Restroom on Spinnaker: In design.
   •    Ohlone Park Restroom: currently working towards 100%
        Construction Documents; plans to submit for permitting this Spring;
        construction anticipated to begin in FY25.
   •    K-Dock Restroom: Project is at 30% Design.

Parks

   •    John Hinkel Park: Complete.
   •    King Pool Tile and Plaster Replacement: Complete.
   •    Grove Park Sports Field Improvements: The field netting and post
        will be installed in late March/early April. Project will open mid-April.
   •    Grove Park 2-5 & 5-12 Playground Renovation: Complete.
   •    Ohlone Park 2-5, 5-12 Play Areas and Mural Garden: Construction
        complete.
