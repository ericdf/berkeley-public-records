         Senior Staﬀ Counsel Ng addressed attendees: Thank you, Jessica.
         I just want to inform the Commission that as of Monday, August 3, BCDC received three
separate anticipated sand mining permit applications.
         The signiﬁcance of these submittals is that BCDC’s regulations prohibiting ex parte
communications has commenced for the projects that are the subjects of these permit applications. As
a reminder, an ex parte communication in this context is any oral or written communication, direct or
indirect, regarding a pending permit application between a Commissioner and either the permit
applicant or a member of the public, that does not occur in a public Commission proceeding.
         As you may also recall, earlier this year some Commissioners previously disclosed certain pre-
application meetings with sand mining representatives out of an abundance of caution, though these
meetings did not technically qualify as prohibited ex parte communications under BCDC’s regulations
because no permit application had yet been submitted.
         I have discussed the agency’s ex parte rules with the sand miners’ legal counsel so I do not
expect that any ex parte communication attempts will be made going forward by him or any
representative on his clients’ behalf towards any Commissioner prior to the Commission considering
these permit applications.
         As a general refresher, after this meeting I will recirculate to the Commission the most current
legal memo summarizing the agency’s ex parte rules, following the conclusion of this meeting.
