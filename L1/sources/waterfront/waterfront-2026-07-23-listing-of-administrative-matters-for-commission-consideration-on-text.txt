                                         Purpose of This Report
The purpose of this report is to inform the Commission about new administrative matters.

        •    The Commission’s rules allow the Executive Director to take action on various
             administrative matters on behalf of the Commission. This report is provided for
             Commissioners and the public to learn about actions the Executive Director
             proposes to take or has recently taken.

        •    This report also details new planning and permitting matters taken by local
             governments in the secondary management area of the Suisun Marsh.

                             Pending Administrative Permit Applications
What is Being Reported Here:
The Executive Director acts on administrative permit applications. Pending administrative
applications are reported here to inform Commissioners and the public and to answer any
questions or concerns about the Executive Director’s determination that a project is “minor” in
scope.

The Commission has two options regarding how to proceed:
   • If the Commission takes no action, the Executive Director will act on these pending
      applications within the deadlines established by state law; or,
   • If a Commissioner objects to the classification of the project as “minor,” and a majority
      of Commissioners agree, a hearing will be scheduled to decide on the proper
      classification of the project.

BCDC Permit Application No. M2026.018.00
             Filed:        July 1, 2026
             90th Day:     September 29, 2026
Location:       Within the Commission’s 100-foot shoreline band jurisdiction, at parking
                lots at the Berkeley Waterfront, in the City of Berkeley, Alameda County.
Description: Implement a limited-term paid parking program with a fee of $1 per hour, $6 per
             day, and $500 per year, to be charged between 9 am and 5 pm daily, consistent
             with Berkeley City Council Resolution No. 71,840-N.S., as approved on June 24,
             2025. The proposed project is a programmatic fee that would apply to existing
             and currently-free public parking spaces along the Bay shoreline, including in the
             South Cove East and J/K Dock parking lots, which are partially within the
             Commission’s 100-foot shoreline band jurisdiction, and in the South Cove West
             lot outside of BCDC jurisdiction. The proposed project does not involve
             construction or alteration of land within BCDC’s 100-foot shoreline band
             jurisdiction and does not involve any work in the Commission’s Bay jurisdiction.
             The paid parking program would include:
                1. Implementation across a total of 273 parking spaces, including 53 spaces
                   within the Commission’s 100-foot shoreline band jurisdiction, representing
                   approximately 20 percent of the total public parking at the Berkeley
                   Waterfront. Free public parking would continue to be available at nearby
                   lots.
                2. Placement of six new pay stations outside the Commission’s jurisdiction.
                   Visitors would pay via payment card at pay stations or using text-to-pay, and
                   cash payments could be made at the Waterfront Office.
                3. Limited-term authorization to allow for evaluation and review of the paid
                   parking program, after which BCDC may grant an extension if the permittee
                   demonstrates that recreational uses have not been significantly impacted.
Note:           The activities described above are for a proposed paid parking program for three
                existing parking lots at the Berkeley Waterfront. The applicant (the City of Berkeley)
                is also a project proponent (along with the San Francisco Bay Area Water
                Emergency Transportation Authority [WETA]) for the Berkeley Water
                Transportation Pier Ferry Project, for which a future BCDC major permit application
                is anticipated.
