Re:              Vision 2050 Revenue Measure Update


This memo provides a brief overview and update on Vision 2050 and the plan to explore
associated infrastructure and affordable housing revenue measure(s).

BACKGROUND
Vision 2050 is a City Council-supported, resident-engaged initiative to address
Berkeley’s $1 billion in infrastructure needs. With voter approval of Measure R, Vision
2050 was defined as engaging residents and experts in developing a 30-year plan to
identify and guide implementation of climate-smart, technologically-advanced,
integrated and efficient infrastructure to support a safe, vibrant and resilient future for
Berkeley. At the same time, our City suffers deep, persistent affordable housing
shortages and a need for greater investment to expand affordable housing.

City Council approved $400,000 of General Fund revenue in the Fiscal Year 2022
budget for the exploration of Vision 2050 Implementation and a potential revenue
measure or measures to increase our infrastructure investment. The City Manager has
convened a working team of residents and City staff to assist with Vision 2050
implementation, and an interdepartmental team to assist with exploration of a revenue
measure or measures for the November 2022 ballot.

UPDATE ON IMPLEMENTATION
In July and August 2021, City staff issued requests for proposals to bring on third-party
experts to support robust public outreach, survey our community on infrastructure
priorities, study the City’s bonding capacity, and develop a long-term program plan that
eliminates deferred maintenance in our public infrastructure by 2050.

The City’s interdepartmental team, along with experts at TBWBH Props and Measures
and Lake Research Partners, is developing a community survey to help understand the
community’s infrastructure and affordable housing priorities. This survey will be issued
in early October and results will return to Council in early winter.
