Good morning, Janelle.

The email serves as the Berkeley CSU/PTRLA SEIU 1021 chapter’s official
request to meet and confer over the City of Berkeley proposed layoffs,
including seeking alternatives to layoffs. The chapter leadership is putting
together a negotiations and can provide available dates later this week.

In the meantime, the Union submitted an RFI on Friday. I believe that some
of those documents were provided to the Union later on Friday, but there
are other requested items that we have not yet received.

Additionally, the Union submits the RFI below. This RFI is meant to focus
less on the specific requirements under the MOU’s layoff language and
looks at the larger budget picture.

   1. The current fund balances for all citywide funds.
   2. A current list of vacancy rates by job title for all citywide positions.
   3. A current list of all vacant positions in SEIU 1021 bargaining units.
   4. Total vacancy savings assumed in the current adopted budget,
      including the total amount of vacancy savings assumed for the
      general fund and for all non-general funds.
   5. The total number of applicants for all city jobs, broken down by job
      classification, for each of the past five fiscal years.
   6. A list of all SEIU Local 1021 represented classifications that have
      experienced the following over the past five fiscal years:
          1. A continuous recruitment
          2. A re-opened recruitment
          3. A failed hiring effort due to declined job offers
          4. A failed hiring effort due to insufficient qualifications
   5. A list of all currently unrepresented classifications of employees in the
      City and the number of FTEs in each classification.
   6. Any report providing demographic information on the city workforce by
      race and gender for each of the last five years, by department,
      classification, and permanent vs temporary status, including
      promotions, terminations, administrative leave, and acting
      assignments to the extent available.
   7. Any analysis or forecasts of property tax revenues, assessment
      appeal refunds or other trends related to property taxes conducted by
      the city or by outside parties for the city
   8. Copies of all financial forecasts that the City is currently utilizing for
      the development of the FY 25-26 and 26-27 budget.
  9. Any analysis or projections of the impact of HR1 on the city budget.
 10. A Copy of the most quarterly or monthly financial statement, including
     unaudited statements.
 11. Copies of all economic reserve policies.
 12. A list of all contributions made into the City’s reserve funds in the
     current fiscal year.
 13. A list of all contract awards over the past five years for services similar
     to those performed by City workers identified for layoff. For each
     contract include,
         1. Name of contractors/supplier
         2. Date of awards
         3. Start and end date of contract
         4. Total amount awarded
         5. Total payments made
         6. Total purchase orders outstanding
         7. Contracting department
         8. Name of any subcontractors
         9. Scope of work
       10. City job classifications that normally perform the work
       11. Reason for contracting out
       12. A current list of all positions in SEIU 1021 bargaining units by
            funding source
