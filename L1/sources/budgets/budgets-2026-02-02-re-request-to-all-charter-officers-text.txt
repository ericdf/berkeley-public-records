Dear Charter Officers,


I'm reaching out regarding the fiscal challenges our city faces in FY 2027 and FY
2028. The General Fund structural deficit is currently estimated at $28 million in FY
2027 and $29 million in FY 2028, amounts that may grow following baseline
adjustments. To address this deficit, the City Manager has asked each department
head to look at 10% reductions to their budgets.


I seek your collaboration and support, as we all must do our part to help balance
and right-size the city budget. After discussions with the Budget and Finance
Committee, I am requesting that all Charter Officers also analyze their FY 2027
and FY 2028 budgets and prepare General Fund reduction plans equal to 10% of
their budgets.


Attached is a copy of the full budget instructions provided to departments for
reference. Once the baseline budget is developed, the City will move into the
budget balancing phase. Below are some key deliverables for this phase in January
2026 that I would like us to align with:


    • Budget Office provides updated FY 2027 and FY 2028 surplus/deficit
      estimates based on updated baseline and General Fund revenue projections
    • Departments must prepare General Fund reduction plans equal to 10% of
      their budgets, net of any revenue loss associated with impacted positions
    • All departments submit revenue proposals and preliminary balancing
      strategies

Please let me know if you or your staff have any questions about these
instructions. I thank you all for helping our city through this challenging time. It's
going to be tough, and I know we can make it through if we work together!
