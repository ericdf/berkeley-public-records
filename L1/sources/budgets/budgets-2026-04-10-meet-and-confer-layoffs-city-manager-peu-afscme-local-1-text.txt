Subject: Demand to Meet and Confer Regarding Proposed Layoffs and Request for Information

Dear Mr. Buddenhagen,

On behalf of AFSCME Council 57, the recognized bargaining representative for employees in the
applicable bargaining unit, this letter serves as a formal demand to meet and confer regarding the
City’s proposed layoffs and/or elimination of positions.

Pursuant to the Meyers-Milias-Brown Act (MMBA) and the applicable Memorandum of
Understanding, the Union demands to bargain over both the decision and the effects of the proposed
layoffs prior to implementation. The proposed action constitutes a change to wages, hours, and other
terms and conditions of employment and therefore triggers the City’s duty to meet and confer in good
faith.

In order for the Union to meaningfully participate in the meet-and-confer process, please provide the
following information:

   1. The number of positions proposed for layoffs, including classification, department, and
      division.
   2. The effective date of the proposed layoffs.
   3. The basis or rationale for the layoffs, including any budget analyses or financial reports relied
      upon.
   4. Current organizational charts for all affected departments.
   5. A list of all current vacancies within the affected classifications and departments.
   6. A list of all temporary, limited-term, contract, or consultant positions performing bargaining-
      unit work.
   7. Overtime reports for the past twelve (12) months for affected departments.
   8. Any plans to redistribute bargaining-unit work or contract out services currently performed by
      bargaining-unit employees.
   9. Any analysis the City conducted regarding alternatives to layoffs.
   10. Seniority lists for affected classifications.

The Union also expects the City to maintain the status quo and refrain from implementing layoffs or
related changes until the parties have completed the meet-and-confer process and any required impasse
procedures.

AFSCME Council 57 remains prepared to meet promptly to discuss alternatives to layoffs, including
but not limited to reassignment to vacant positions, voluntary separation incentives, hiring freezes,
reductions in overtime, and other measures designed to mitigate or avoid layoffs.

Please contact me no later than April 16, 2026, to schedule the initial meet-and-confer session and to
confirm when the requested information will be produced.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
