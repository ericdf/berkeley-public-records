               BUDGET BALANCING OPTION:
            W O R K E R C O M P E N S AT I O N H O L I D AY
 FY26 includes holiday of $5.5M GF ($10.7M All Funds)

 Used to balance and fund other needs (TI construction costs, Marina Dock project) for
  the past 2 years

• Assets have gone from $56M to $35M (6/30/25 projected)

• Assets / Liabilities have gone from 100% to 68%, and another year of “holiday” could
  reduce this to 55%

• Actuarial recommend an asset balance of $51.3M at 6/30/25 to have 80% confidence
  that our total liability is covered.

• Short-term costs of payment of salaries while on leave and overtime to backfill in public
  safety as well as long term liability depending on length and cost of settlement
