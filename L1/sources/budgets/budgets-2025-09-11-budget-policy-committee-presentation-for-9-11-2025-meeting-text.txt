                                                                      FY 27 – FY 28 Biennial Budget Roadmap
Overview
    • City has relied upon one-time actions to address its deficit and implement new initiatives for
      many years
        – Reserves
        – Assumed vacancy savings / positions held vacant
        – One time grant resources
        – Project deferrals

    • FY 26 Mid-Biennial Budget no different than the past -- shortfalls addressed through
      one-time measures

    • FY 27–FY 28 Biennial Budget estimated $28M structural deficit

    • Goal: Align expenditures with available resources
        – Cost reductions
        – Revenue increases
        – Efficiencies

                                                                                                                                   FY 27 – FY 28 Biennial Budget Roadmap
FY 26 Balancing Measures
    • $7.5M increase in revenues
    • 44.4 vacant positions
    • 11.8 positions reallocated from the General Fund to other funds
    • One (1) provisional temporary position eliminated
    • $6.0M Section 115 Trust Funds for pension obligations
    • $5.2M Workers Compensation Fund transfer to General Fund
    • $6.2M IT Cost Allocation Plan transfer to the General Fund
    • $2.5M of Measure U1 resources
