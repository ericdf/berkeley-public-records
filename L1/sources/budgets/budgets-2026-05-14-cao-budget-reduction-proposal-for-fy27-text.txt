Dear Mayor Ishii and Members of the Berkeley City Council:



On April 23, 2026, The City Attorney’s Office (CAO) staff made a budget presentation to the
Budget and Finance Committee, detailing allocated and actual spending for FY25/26 and
presented a budget proposal for FY27/28. The presentation reflected the CAO’s efforts to
reduce the non-personnel budget but did not include any personnel reductions beyond the
one Senior Legal Secretary position that was reduced in establishing the FY27 baseline
budget.

At the meeting, members of the Committee requested more details on the budget
reductions and that the CAO explore further reductions. Below, the CAO details reductions
made in non-personnel spending and proposes eliminating an additional existing staff
position (Paralegal) to further reduce personnel spending.

Of significant importance in understanding the CAO budget through FY25/26 and heading
into FY27/28, is that the funding needed for outside legal and professional services (nearly
1.4 million dollars spent in each FY25 and FY26) was funded using salary savings and roll
up cost savings from the CAO budget in previous years. Due to the volume and complexity
of recent and ongoing litigation this level of spending with outside counsel will continue
into the new fiscal year and the CAO does not expect to have sufficient savings from FY26
to roll into FY27 as we have in years past. Therefore, in establishing the FY27 baseline
budget, the CAO included $1.3 million in funding for outside legal and professional
services. As we near the end of FY26, we will have a better understanding of any potential
savings that may be carried over into the next budget cycle and will report this back to
Council, but current projections indicated that we will have far less than what we were
able to carry-over in the last two years to fund these services.

Since the April 23, 2026 Budget and Finance Committee meeting, the CAO has proposed
eliminating one of only two Paralegal positions to help meet budget reduction goals. As
explained more fully below, while there is concern that eliminating this position could
increase our dependence on outside legal and professional services, potentially at a higher
cost, we are prepared to make this reduction if Council requires it. In the table below, the
CAO shows reductions from the FY27 baseline budget totaling 4.3%, exclusive of funding
for outside legal and professional services.

CAO offers this additional potential personnel reduction with great reservation and a
significant note of caution. This proposal would eliminate a currently filled paralegal
position and maintain a vacancy in the Senior Legal Secretary role. This would result in
critical understaffing for CAO, which relies on paralegals and legal secretaries to, among
many specialized tasks, prepare mandatory court tables and appendices, exhibits, and
discovery requests, draft declarations, review and produce litigation and PRA documents,
respond to subpoenas, set up depositions and prepare witness exhibits, file documents
with the court and deliver mandatory courtesy copies, track court deadlines and save
documents in our files, and handle and track a high volume of administrative claims.

If a paralegal position were eliminated, CAO would be left with just two legal secretaries
and one paralegal to support approximately 90 lawsuits a year and hundreds more claims
and PRAs. This would have a significant negative impact on CAO’s ability to deliver high-
quality legal services to the City and impose even greater burdens on an already-stretched
team of lawyers who will be taken away from critical advice work to engage in
administrative activities that they are currently not trained to perform.

Without adequate in-house resources to support litigation and claims processing, CAO
may be unable to effectively represent the City in matters of great importance and
potential fiscal impact. As that would be an unacceptable result, making these cuts could,
perversely, require CAO to spend additional funds on outside resources, including by
leveraging outside counsel’s support staff.
