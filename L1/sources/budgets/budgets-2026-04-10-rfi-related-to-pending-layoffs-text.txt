As of yet the Union has not received any of that information.

Additionally, upon review of the layoff language in the MOU, the Union is
making this initial RFI for information necessary to enforce the contract.
  1. Notice to the Union the stated reason for the layoff. The MOU
      lists; “Material change in duties and organization, adverse working
      conditions, return of employee from leave of absence, or shortage of
      work or funds.”
  2. The cost savings the City intends to receive through the layoff
      action.
  3. List of all frozen vacancies. The MOU states the City shall, “freeze
      all current City vacancies in the competitive service in similar and
      related classifications to those likely to be targeted for layoff, as well
      as all related full-time, benefited, temporary positions which are
      expected to last six (6) months or more.”
  4. Seniority Service Dates. The Union requests the Citywide SSD,
      along with the specific lists associated with the MOU, “Human
      Resources Department will immediately establish separate
      Probationary and Permanent Seniority Lists for each classification
      targeted for layoff.”
  5. List of “natural progression” and “normal progression”
      classifications for all impacted classifications. The MOU uses
      both terms to define the parameters for Retreat rights. While the
      Union may disagree with included classifications, we request the City
      provide us with the City’s position on both progressions.
  6. Amount designated for Outplacement. The MOU states, “City
      Manager may authorize the Human Resources Department to offer
      the affected employee the option of out placement. The out placement
      offer would provide a designated amount of funds to be paid to the
      employee for use by the employee for career development” and puts
      the range of outplacement payment at $1k-30k.
  7. List of all emergency, temporary, intermittent, seasonal,
      provisional, etc employees.
   8. Copies of notices sent to all employees listed in #7. The MOU
      states, “56.5.1 Emergency, temporary, intermittent, seasonal, etc.,
      employees shall be notified individually, in writing, of pending layoff as
      soon as possible. However, at least two (2) weeks notification is
      desirable if possible. 56.5.2 Provisional employees shall be notified
      individually, in writing, of pending layoff as soon as possible, with no
      less than fifteen (15) calendar days notification if targeted for release
      or reassignment.”
   9. Copies of notices sent to all permanent, probationary, and
      career-exempt employees. The MOU states, “Permanent,
      probationary, and career-exempt employees shall be notified
      individually, in writing, of pending layoffs as soon as possible, with no
      less than thirty (30) calendar days notification if targeted for release or
      reassignment.”

As you know, the Berkeley CSU layoff language is quite expansive and the
Union continues to comb through it to identify any other information we
need at this point. In addition to any other RFIs we submit related the MOU
language, the Union also plans to submit an RFI in order to carry out the
obligation to meet and confer over the impacts of layoff, including seeking
alternatives. We are working with our research team to finalize that list and
imagine much of it will be released on the 14th.

Please let us know if you have any questions.
