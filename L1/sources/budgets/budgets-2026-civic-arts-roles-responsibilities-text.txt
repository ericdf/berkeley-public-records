Mark Salinas – Civic Arts Coordinator (Public Art Lead)
Mark serves as the functional lead for the City’s Public Art Program and supports implementation of Berkeley’s
public art initiatives, including capital-project-related public art coordination, temporary installations,
exhibitions, and artwork conservation.

Public Art Program Management
    Lead management of the City’s Public Art Program
    Staff lead for the Civic Arts Public Art Subcommittee
    Management of public art projects associated with City capital improvement projects
    Oversight of rotating, temporary, and one-time public art installations on City property
    Management of public art collections, conservation, and maintenance planning

Project Development and Implementation
    Coordination of artist recruitment, artist selection processes, and artwork commissioning
    Management of site selection, project scoping, design development, fabrication, installation, and
       closeout
    Coordination with architects, engineers, contractors, fabricators, conservators, artists, and City
       departments
    Monitoring of project schedules, budgets, milestones, and implementation risks
    Identification of project issues and coordination of solutions to address schedule, budget, or
       implementation challenges

Commission, Community, and Department Coordination
    Preparation of materials and presentations related to public art projects for Civic Arts Commission
     review and approval
    Coordination of public art community engagement, publicity, and dedication events
    Collaboration with departments to integrate public art into City infrastructure projects
    Representation of the City in meetings with community partners, departments, boards, commissions,
     artists, and project teams

Public Art Quality, Maintenance, and Stewardship
    Oversight of artwork design, fabrication, installation, conservation, and maintenance
    Review of materials, techniques, and long-term maintainability of public artworks
      Coordination of ongoing maintenance and conservation plans
      Research, data gathering, and reporting related to public art, site context, materials, and assigned
