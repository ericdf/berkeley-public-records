               BUDGET BALANCING OPTION:
            W O R K E R C O M P E N S AT I O N H O L I D AY
 The City is self-insured for worker compensation claims.
 All City funds contribute to the worker compensation fund.
 This approach means that the General Fund would not contribute to the fund for FY26.
 With FY25 holiday, expected to end FY 2025 with $35-36M in assets.
 City actuaries expect our discounted total outstanding liability to be $44.3M.
 75% confidence in being able to cover liabilities (their minimum recommended level),
  they calculate that we have $49.6M in assets. 70% confidence level to be marginally
  acceptable, and for that level we would need $48.1M in assets.
 For FY26, a $5M transfer to General Fund (leaving a net contribution of around $6M to
  the Workers Comp Fund), plus $1M in earned interest, and paying out an estimated
  $8.4M in claims during FY 2026, we would have approximately $33.6M – $34.6M at
  6/30/26. Our actuaries have not delivered a report giving our estimated liabilities with
  confidence levels for the end of FY 2026 but based on previous asset level, we will be
  less than 70% confidence level.
