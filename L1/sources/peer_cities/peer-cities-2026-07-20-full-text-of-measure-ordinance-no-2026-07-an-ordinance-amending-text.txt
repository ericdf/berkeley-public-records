        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 4 SECTION 4-7 OF THE ALBANY

     “4-7.1          Title; Declaration of Findings and Purpose.
23
              This section may be cited as the "Emergency Medical Services, Advanced Life Support,
24

25   and Fire Protection Tax". The special tax imposed under this section is solely for the purpose

26   of raising revenue for paramedic, advanced life support, firefighting services, firefighting

27   equipment and ambulance service. This section is not enacted for regulatory purposes.

     The people of the City find and declare that:
 1
     a.     The continuing provision of the City's Emergency Medical Services, Advanced Life
 2
     Support, and Fire Protection Programs is a matter of the highest and most urgent necessity.
 3
     b.     In the last two decades, the City’s population has grown more than twice as fast as the
 4

 5   number of residences, placing increased demand on emergency medical services without an

 6   increase in tax revenue to support those services.

 7   c.     The cost for the City to provide fire protection, advanced life support and emergency
 8
     transport services exceeds the revenues generated from fees for providing such services, the
 9
     Emergency Medical Services Special Tax and the Paramedic Advanced Life Support, Fire
10
     Engine and Ambulance Service Special Tax. The purpose of this special tax is to fund
11
     emergency transport costs and replace the two aforementioned special taxes and continue to
12

13   provide funding for the City's Emergency Medical Services, Advanced Life Support, and Fire

14   Protection Programs.
15   d.     Adoption of this special tax by a two-thirds vote helps assure that the City can continue
16
     to provide, and improve, the Emergency Medical Services, Advanced Life Support, and Fire
17
     Protection Programs, and avoid any severe adverse impact on the health and safety of Albany
18
     residents.
