SUBJECT:              Televising the City-Initiated Waterfront Planning Consultant
                      Selection Process

FROM:             Jacqueline L. Bucholz, City Clerk
________________________________________________________________________

RECOMMENDATION:

Approve the televising of the City-Initiated Waterfront Planning Consultant Selection
Process, which is scheduled for October 4, 2006, at 7:30 p.m. in the Council Chamber.

BACKGROUND

The City Council authorized staff to facilitate the Planning & Zoning Commission
sponsorship of a City-Initiated Waterfront Planning Process in consultation with other
City Commissions/Committees.

Based on Council action, staff has prepared and distributed a Request for Qualifications
for planning consultant services for Albany Waterfront Planning of the Golden Gate
Fields property.

A public hearing has been scheduled for October 4, 2006, at 7:30 p.m. in the Council
Chamber and it is recommended that this hearing be televised, as many Albany residents
are interested in this matter.

Council will be receiving a recommendation in the near future from the Social &
Economic Justice Commission regarding which City Commissions/Committees should be
televised.

Staff is recommending that this public hearing be televised and if a part-time employee is
used the cost would be $20 per hour and if a full-time supervisor is needed the costs
would increase.

FINANCIAL IMPACT

Employee costs are $20 per hour for a part-time employee and additional costs if a full-
time supervisor were needed.
