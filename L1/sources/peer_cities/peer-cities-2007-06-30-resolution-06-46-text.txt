 4          WHEREAS, Caruso Affiliated has indicated its intention to submit a

 5   development application for a mixed-use development at the Golden Gate Fields

 6   property.

 7          WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the community to maximize public

 8   input into the review of the application that is expected to be submitted by Caruso

 9   Affiliated.

10          WHEREAS, there has been much discussion about an appropriate

11   planning process for Golden Gate Fields property.

12          WHEREAS, it is important and valuable for the City, the community, the

13   property owner, and the developer to establish certainty with regard to how the

14   planning process will proceed.

15          WHEREAS, the City has limited financial resources to conduct studies

16   that will provide in-depth environmental review of the Golden Gate Fields

17   property and it is in the best interest of the community to conduct these studies

18   without burdening our city’s taxpayers.

19          WHEREAS, the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) process

20   for reviewing projects is a well-established and effective way of informing the

21   public and the decision makers of the impacts of a proposed project.

22          WHEREAS, the CEQA process allows for the study of numerous

23   alternatives and provides the opportunity for the City Council to choose from

24   amongst those Alternatives.

25          WHEREAS, the CEQA process will be conducted at the expense of the

26   developer and not the Albany taxpayer, though the study will be conducted under

27   the direction of the City of Albany.

28          WHEREAS, the result of any environmental review of a project at Golden

29   Gate Fields will be subject to review by the public, city commissions, and the City
 1   Council, and the project will ultimately be subjected to a vote of the people of
 2   Albany as required by Measure C.
 3          WHEREAS, Caruso Affiliated has agreed to pay for a city planning
 4   process that will be conducted by the City of Albany to identify two of the
 5   Alternatives to be studied as part of the EIR of the Caruso Affiliated proposal.
 6          NOW THEREFORE, THE ALBANY CITY COUNCIL RESOLVES THAT:
 7   If, within the next 60 days, Caruso Affiliated submits a development application for the
 8   Golden Gate Fields property, the City Council directs staff to accept and process the
 9   application including completion of any required CEQA review, in compliance with all
10   applicable state laws and municipal ordinances. At the completion of this process staff is
11   directed to present to the City Council a completed Final EIR so that the City Council can
12   consider certification of it in compliance with CEQA and can consider whether to place
13   the project on the ballot for final consideration by the voters in accordance with Measure
14   C. Staff is also directed to develop, with Caruso Affiliated, a timeframe and approach for
15   a city planning process for the development of two project alternatives to be studied as
16   part of the EIR, as agreed to in the Caruso Affiliated letter dated July 7, 2006.
