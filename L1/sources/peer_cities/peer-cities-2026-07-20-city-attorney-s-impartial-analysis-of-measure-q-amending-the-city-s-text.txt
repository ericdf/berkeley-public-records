                       CITY OF ALBANY
     CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE “Q”
 AMENDING THE CITY’S SPECIAL PARCEL TAX TO FUND EMERGENCY
MEDICAL SERVICES, ADVANCED LIFE SUPPORT AND FIRE PROTECTION
                   SERVICES (EMS/ALS/FIRE)

In 2022, Albany voters approved Measure “K”, a special parcel tax on property within
the City to fund emergency medical, advanced life support and fire protection services
(EMS/ALS/Fire). The parcel tax is currently levied annually on residential and
commercial properties at a rate of $0.0807 per lot square foot, as defined in the Measure.
It allows annual rate adjustments subject to a 5% cap, and incorporates both a 100%
exemption for residential parcels owned by qualifying very low-income residents and a
100% rebate to qualifying very low-income renters of tax imposed on a rental unit where
they reside, as defined therein.

On July 20, 2026, the City Council voted to place Measure “Q” on the November 3,
2026 General Municipal Election ballot. If approved by two-thirds (2/3) of Albany
voters, this Measure would amend the tax as follows:

▪      Retain the 100% exemption for residential parcels owned by qualifying very low-
       income residents, and add a 50% exemption for qualifying low-income resident
       owners; and

▪      Retain the 100% rebate to qualifying very low-income renters of tax imposed on a
       unit where they reside, and add a 50% rebate to similarly qualifying low-income
       renters, as defined therein; and

▪      Maintain annual Consumer Price Index (CPI) adjustments, but with a 3% cap
       instead of 5%; and

▪      Maintain the rate at $0.0807 per lot square foot, annually adjusted by CPI, unless
       any parcel 500,000 square feet or larger is sold, leased, or transferred to make it
       tax exempt, at which point the maximum rates shall be:

       ◦       $0.254 per building square foot for developed properties; and

       ◦       $0.110 per lot square foot for undeveloped parcels; both annually adjusted
               by CPI.

▪      Clarify that this tax applies to possessory interests (i.e. leaseholds) on a parcel
       where the owner is exempt from parcel taxation.

The Council may levy the tax at a lower rate in any year and restore it back to a prior
rate, provided the maximum voter approved rates aren’t exceeded.

The tax is estimated to generate $2,500,000 annually until repealed by voters at a
subsequent election. The tax will continue to be deposited into a dedicated fund to
provide paramedic, advanced life support, firefighting services, firefighting equipment
and ambulance services. This parcel tax would be collected the same and be subject to
the same requirements as other property taxes collected by the County of Alameda on
behalf of the City of Albany.

This Measure would continue requiring the City’s Finance Director to prepare an annual
accountability report showing: (i) parcel tax revenues collected and spent; and (ii) status
of projects or description of any programs funded with tax revenues.

A “yes” vote on Measure “Q” will approve this amendment to the EMS/ALS/Fire
parcel tax.

A “no” vote on Measure “Q” will not approve this amendment to the EMS/ALS/Fire
parcel tax.

The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure “Q”. If you desire a copy
of the Measure, please call the Office of the City Clerk at (510) 528-5710 and a copy
will be mailed at no cost to you.
