          From: City Manager

          on the upcoming community survey as well as any other proposed measures.
          Financial Implications: None
          Contact: Scott Ferris, Parks, Recreation and Waterfront, (510) 981-6700, Peter
          Radu, City Manager's Office, (510) 981-7000, Anne Cardwell, City Manager's Office,
          (510) 981-7000
          Action: 6 speakers. The Council provided direction on the survey to:
           • Survey for Parks Tax increases of 20% and 28%.
           • Add a question about general support for a revenue measure to support local
              arts organizations and programs.
           • Survey for a Measure P extension with two options: 1) renewing the current tax;
              and 2) adding tiers for higher value sales (3% at $3 million; 3.5% at $10 million).

              inclusion in the survey.
          The Council also provided general direction to develop a ballot measure to extend
          the Sugar-Sweetened Beverage Tax.

2.        Council Referral – Request for City Council to place a limited Library tax on the
          November 2024 ballot
          From: Board of Library Trustees
          Recommendation: Adopt a resolution to include on the November 2024 ballot a
          measure levying a new, limited tax in order to maintain current levels of Library
          services, building maintenance, collections and staffing. The proposed rate is 6¢ per
          square foot of residential building space and 9¢ of commercial building space, with
          annual adjustments, and exempting low-income homeowners. The recommendation
          is to place this measure on the ballot, using language that aligns as closely as
          possible with the language employed by Godbe Research in the March 2024
          community survey: “To maintain Berkeley Public Library services/ facilities such as: -
          summer reading programs for children; - keeping qualified librarians; - quality places
          for children; - homework help/ tutoring; - book, material/ digital collections; - maintain
          library facilities; - senior/ accessible programs; and - maintain weekday and weekend
          hours; shall the Berkeley Public Library measure be adopted, levying 6¢ per square
          foot of residential building space (other rates detailed in the voter handbook),
          providing $5,600,000 annually, until ended by voters, with annual adjustments, and
          exempting very low-income homeowners?”
          Financial Implications: See report
          Contact: Tess Mayer, Commission Secretary, (510) 981-6100
          Action: 9 speakers. M/S/C (Arreguin/Hahn) to refer to the City Attorney to work with
          Library staff to develop a revenue measure for the November 2024 ballot.
          Vote: All Ayes.
