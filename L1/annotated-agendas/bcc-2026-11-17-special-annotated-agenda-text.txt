Action Calendar – New Business

1.       Amending Resolution No. 71,513-N.S. Encampment Policy Resolution to
         Promote Healthy and Safe Neighborhoods to Include Prohibition Against
         Encampments in Close Proximity to Preschools and In-Home Early Childhood
         Daycare Facilities
         From: Councilmember Kesarwani (Author), Councilmember Blackaby (Co-
         Sponsor), Councilmember Taplin (Co-Sponsor)
         Recommendation: Approve adoption of a new resolution amending the
         Encampment Policy to Promote Healthy and Safe Neighborhoods and rescinding
         Resolution No. 71,513-N.S. Recommend the new resolution adds proximity to
         licensed preschools and/or in-home early childhood daycare facilities to the list of
         circumstances in which the City Manager is authorized to close an encampment
         even when an offer of shelter is not practicable.
         Specifically, it is recommended to add a 600foot buffer zone prohibiting
         encampments around preschools and in-home early childhood daycare facilities in
         order to safeguard the health and welfare of young children. Preschool-aged children
         are particularly vulnerable to a range of public health concerns because they are
         more likely to play closer to the ground, touching potentially infected objects and
         then putting their hands in their mouth. A buffer zone around these institutions
         should be adopted to minimize public health and safety risks. Resolution No. 71,513-
         N.S should add to the list of enforcement circumstances:
         -The encampment is located within 600 feet of any licensed preschool and/or in-
         home early childhood daycare facility as measured by a straight line from the
         nearest point of the property line of the parcel on which the preschool/in-home early
         childhood daycare facility is located to the nearest point of the encampment,
         including its associated belongings.
         Further, refer to the City Manager to amend Administrative Regulation 10.2 to clarify
         that Temporary Non-Commercial (TNC) objects are prohibited within 600 feet of a
         licensed preschool/in-home early childhood daycare facility.
         Financial Implications: Staff time
         Contact: Rashi Kesarwani, Councilmember, District 1, (510) 981-7110
         Action: 60 speakers. M/S/Failed (Lunaparra/Tregub) to amend the main motion to
         change the buffer zone to 300 feet.
         Vote: Ayes – Tregub, Lunaparra; Noes – Kesarwani, Taplin, Bartlett, O’Keefe,
         Blackaby, Humbert, Ishii.

         Action: M/S/C (Kesarwani/Taplin) to:
         1. Adopt Resolution No. 72,502–N.S. as revised in Supplemental Communications
         Packet #2 and further revised at the meeting to add a Resolved clause to rescind the
         previous resolution.
         2. Approve recommendation to refer amendments to AR 10.2 to the City Manager.
         Vote: Ayes – Kesarwani, Taplin, Bartlett, Tregub, O’Keefe, Blackaby, Humbert; Noes
         – Lunaparra, Ishii.
