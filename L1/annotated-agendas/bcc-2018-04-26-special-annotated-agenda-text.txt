1.      Proposed Policies on Sidewalks and Encampments
        From: Mayor Arreguin and Councilmember Hahn
        Recommendation: Direct the City Manager to return to Council with (1) an
        Ordinance codifying Regulations for Sitting, Lying, Dogs and Objects on Sidewalks
        and in Parklets, (2) a final Encampment Response Policy, and (3) necessary
        amendments, if any, to existing policies, Administrative Regulations and/or code
        sections impacted by the proposed Ordinance and Policy, for Council adoption.
        Financial Implications: Staff time
        Contact: Jesse Arreguin, Mayor, 981-7100
        Action: 70 speakers. M/S/C (Maio/Arreguin) to direct the City Manager to return to
        Council with (1) an Ordinance for Council adoption codifying Regulations for Sitting,
        Lying, Dogs and Objects on Sidewalks and in Parklets; and (2) necessary
        amendments, if any, to existing policies, Administrative Regulations and/or code
        sections impacted by the proposed Ordinance. Regulations for Council adoption to
        be based on the revised materials submitted at the meeting and further amended
        as below.
           3.a. BART Access Corridor is a Sidewalk, including a wide plaza area, on the same
            side of the street as a BART Station entrance, within 25 feet of such entrance and the
            west side of Shattuck Avenue from Addison Street to Alston Way.
           3.c. Parklet is an improved area within the dedicated public right-of-way with seating,
            tables, landscaping and/or other amenities, that is intended for being used temporarily
            for the enjoyment and use of all citizens. [Definition to conform with proposed code
            amendments]
           Direction to staff to verify if the Ohlone Greenway is covered under the policy for City
            Parks or if it should be covered under these regulations
           9. Lying – first sentence should not be included in the ordinance language.
           9.iii. iii)  On Sidewalks in all Commercial Districts and in the Manufacturing (M),
            Mixed Manufacturing (MM) and Mixed-Use Light Industrial Zones (MULI) between the
            hours of 7:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. Monday through Saturday, and 10 a.m. and 10:00
            p.m. on Sundays and federal holidays. (Lying is permitted in these locations from
            10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. Monday through Saturday, and 6:00 p.m. Sunday to 7:00
            a.m. Monday.)
           10.b. Cushioning Material shall not expand beyond what is reasonably needed to
            cushion the body while seated or lying, and shall be removed by the owner when not
            in use.
           In all sections related to dogs, clarify that the dogs must be leashed.
           In all sections where it states “”action requested” change to “action required.”
           12. Enforcement – staff to analyze which staff should address these regulations and
            determine if any specificity is required in the regulations.
           12.a.i. If, based on complaints, direct observation or referrals, City staff become aware
            that Objects or Cushioning Materials are placed in a location or manner in violation of
            these or other regulations, written notice shall be provided stating the City’s
            regulations and the corrective action required. If Objects or Cushioning Materials are
            unattended notice shall be posted on or directly adjacent to such Objects or
            Cushioning Materials.
           12.a.ii. Depending on the impacts of the violation, notice shall state whether corrective
            action must be taken immediately, or may specify any time period, not to exceed 1
            hour, for Objects or Cushioning Material to be moved and brought into compliance
            with these regulations.
           12.a.iv. – Deleted
           Remove references to “City staff” throughout and just use “City.”
           12.b.ii. If the individual does not come into compliance after being provided with notice
            and an opportunity to comply, a Citation may be issued.
