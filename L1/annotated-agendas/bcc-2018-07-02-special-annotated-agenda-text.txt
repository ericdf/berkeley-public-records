1. Council Policy: Suspend Enforcement of Vehicle Lodging/Camping at Hs
   Lordships Parking Lot, 199 Seawall Drive
   From: Mayor Arreguin and Councilmembers Davila and Harrison
   Recommendation: 1) Consistent with Resolution No. 68,206-N.S. “Extension of
   Declaration of Homeless Shelter Crisis”, adopt as an official City of Berkeley policy that
   enforcement of Penal Code Section 647(e) “Lodging on Public Property” and B.M.C.
   Section 6.20.260 “Camping Prohibited” is suspended for up to 50 vehicles
   lodging/camping in the parking lot at Hs Lordships Restaurant, 199 Seawall Drive for a
   period of one month commencing July 2, 2018 and directing the City Manager to take
   action to implement this Council policy. Council may extend this policy by majority vote
   for another month following July 31, 2018; 2) Direct the City Manager to install portable
   restroom(s) and dumpster(s) in the Hs Lordships Parking Lot utilizing a portion of the
   $400,000 allocated by the City Council on June 26, 2018 for “Portable
   Restrooms/Garbage Service Near Encampments & Addressing Illegal Dumping”;
   3) Direct the City Manager to negotiate with Lisa Bullwinkel/Another Bullwinkel Show,
   contractor for the 4th of July Festival and Berkeley Kite Festival, and upon mutual
   agreement of the parties, permit existing vehicles parked in Hs Lordships Parking Lot to
   remain parked on July 4, 2018 and July 28-29, 2018. All vehicles shall pay the required
   parking fee per vehicle.
   Financial Implications: Staff time and costs for portable restroom and dumpster
   service.
   Contact: Jesse Arreguin, Mayor, 981-7100

   Action: M/S/Failed (Arreguin/Davila) to approve the recommendation with the following
   amendments:
       The number of permitted vehicles is limited to 33.
       The permitted vehicles will pay the event fee of $20 for a car and $50 for a vehicle
         over 17 feet for the July 4th Event and the Kite Festival.
       Permitted vehicles will be limited to a specified area.
       Security for the Hs Lordships building will be provided by the City.
       The process to obtain a permit will be on a first come-first served basis.
       The City will provide more than one portable toilet, a handwashing station, and a
         dumpster.
   Vote: Ayes – Davila, Bartlett, Harrison, Arreguin; Noes – Worthington; Abstain – Maio;
   Absent – Hahn, Wengraf, Droste.

   Action: M/S/Carried (Harrison/Arreguin) to suspend enforcement of state and city laws
   related to person lodging and dwelling in their vehicles at 199 Seawall Drive through July
   8, 2018, and require that vehicles in the lot pay the event parking fee for July 4 th.
   Vote: Ayes – Maio, Davila, Bartlett, Harrison, Arreguin; Noes – Worthington; Abstain –
   None; Absent – Hahn, Wengraf, Droste.
