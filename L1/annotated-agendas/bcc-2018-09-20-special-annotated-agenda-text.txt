1.     ZAB Appeal: 749 Contra Costa Avenue
       From: City Manager
       Recommendation: Conduct a public hearing and upon conclusion, adopt a
       Resolution to affirm the Zoning Adjustments Board decision, with revisions to the
       findings and conditions of approval (as set forth in Attachment 1) to address the
       appellant’s enforcement and verification concerns; to approve Use Permit
       #ZP2016-0133 to construct an approximately 520-square-foot addition on an
       existing 2,257-square-foot, three-story, single-family dwelling; and dismiss the
       appeal.
       Financial Implications: None
       Contact: Timothy Burroughs, Planning and Development, 981-7400

       Public Testimony: The Mayor opened the public hearing. 15 speakers.
       M/S/C (Wengraf/Maio) to close the public hearing.
       Vote: Ayes – Maio, Davila, Harrison, Hahn, Wengraf, Worthington, Droste,
       Arreguin; Noes – None; Abstain – None; Absent – Bartlett.

       Action: M/S/C (Wengraf/Arreguin) to adopt Resolution No. 68,600–N.S. affirming
       the Zoning Adjustments Board decision, with revisions to the findings and
       conditions of approval (as set forth in Attachment 1) to address the appellant’s
       enforcement and verification concerns; to approve Use Permit #ZP2016-0133 to
       construct an approximately 520-square-foot addition on an existing 2,257-square-
       foot, three-story, single-family dwelling with amendments noted below; and dismiss
       the appeal.
           Condition #11 wording is changed from “adequate privacy screening” to
               “solid, opaque, and permanent wall”
           In condition #26 the monitoring language is amended to include “on-site”
               monitoring as is required in condition #14
           To make the corresponding changes to the Findings as follows: Windows
               from the proposed project are screened from view from neighbors to the rear
               and north by mature vegetation. To ensure privacy to the southern neighbor,
               the project is approved with frosted glass windows on the southern façade
               and the southern facing bathroom room windows is also conditioned to
               which shall also be fixed. A solid, opaque, and permanent wall will also be
               provided along the southern side of the master bedroom balcony.
       Vote: Ayes – Maio, Hahn, Wengraf, Droste, Arreguin; Noes – Worthington; Abstain
       – Davila, Harrison; Absent – Bartlett.

       Recess 7:20 p.m. – 7:48 p.m.
