Subject:         Proposed FY 2027 and FY 2028 Biennial Budget

RECOMMENDATION
Request that the Budget & Finance Policy Committee receive, review, and discuss the
City Manager’s Proposed FY 2027 and FY 2028 Biennial Budget.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
The Proposed Budget outlines planned citywide expenditures for Fiscal Years 2027 and
2028, including General Fund (GF) expenditures. Fiscal impacts may change based on
Committee and Council direction and will be finalized with budget adoption on June 23,
2026.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The Proposed Biennial Budget addresses the projected deficits and includes the
proposed budget balancing plan, projected revenues, proposed expenditures; and
proposed staffing levels.

Several staff presentations to the Budget and Finance Policy Committee informed the
development of the Proposed FY 2027 & FY 2028 Biennial Budget:

      •   FY 27 – FY 28 Biennial Budget Process and Overview1
      •   Parking Funds Update 2
      •   Marina Fund Update3
