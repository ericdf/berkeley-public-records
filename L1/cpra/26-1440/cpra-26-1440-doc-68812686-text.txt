                                RECAP

 FY 2026 Increases vs FY 2025 largely one-time transactions (Transfers, Successor
  Agency Residuals), plus organic growth in real property valuations
 Increases to FY 2026 Projections from Transfer Tax, TOT (Hotel) Tax, Successor
  Agency Residuals
 For FY 2027/28 and beyond, we expect continued organic growth plus increases
  from return of tax deductions to General Fund from Successor Agency
 Projections are made with the best information available at the time. Changes in
  economic conditions can occur without much warning, but staff will continue to
  monitor data and will update projections if material changes occur.
