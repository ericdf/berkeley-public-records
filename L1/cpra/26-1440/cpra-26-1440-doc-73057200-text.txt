                        Impact on Current Contracted Providers

•   A fiscal and programmatic review of all currently funded MHSA programs will
    be conducted to determine whether they meet BHSA eligibility requirements.

• Some contracted (or new) programs that primarily meet BHSA eligibility, may
  also require new Request for Proposal (RFP) processes. If needed, the RFP
  process will take place within the FY26 winter/spring timeframe.

• Community partners, City staff and stakeholders will be informed of the review
  process.

        Community Participation in BHSA Integrated Plan Process


There will be multiple opportunities for the community to participate in the BHSA
Integrated Planning Process and inform the BHSA Integrated Plan, including the
following:

•   Fill out the BHSA Integrated Plan Survey
•   Attend a Community Input Meeting or Focus Group
•   Review and provide input on the written Draft BHSA Integrated Plan
•   Attend the Public Hearing on the Draft BHSA Integrated Plan

Detailed information on all opportunities will be widely distributed soon.

Public Health
Role of a Local Health Jurisdiction
Local health jurisdictions (LHJs), such as city or county health departments, are the public health
frontline and are essential for protecting and promoting the well-being of a community. At the local
level, LHJs can tailor public health initiatives to the specific needs of their populations and respond to
issues in a timely and effective manner.
Berkeley Focus
• Complementary to Alameda County
• Respond to, and influence, social determinants of health
• Continuous workforce development, including student interns from
  UC Berkeley and Berkeley City College

Legal Authority and Administration
• Legal requirements for LHJs in CA primarily established in the CA
  Health and Safety Code, and CA Code of Regulations
• Must meet legal requirements to receive funding allocations
• Legal requirements include core functions: communicable disease
  control, emergency preparedness, environmental health, community
  health assessments, vital records, and health equity
