Balancing the FY 2027 & FY 2028 Biennial Budget
As previously noted, the City faces significant fiscal challenges in this biennial budget cycle, with a
projected structural General Fund deficit that are currently estimated at $28 million in FY 2027 and $29
million in FY 2028, figures that may grow following baseline adjustments. Balancing rising expenditures
against limited revenues remains a difficult task. With this in mind, once the baseline budget is
developed, the City will move into budget balancing phase.
Some key dates and deliverables for this phase are:
January 2026
•        • Budget Office provides updated FY 2027 and FY 2028 surplus/deficit estimates based on
updated baseline and General Fund revenue projection.
•        • Departments must prepare General Fund reduction plans equal to 10% of their budgets, net
of any revenue loss associated with impacted positions.
•        • Departments managing other funds that are projecting deficits must submit reduction plans
sufficient to fully balance each fund in FY 2027. Target reductions will vary based on the size of each
fund’s projected shortfall.
•        • All departments submit revenue proposals and preliminary balancing strategies.
