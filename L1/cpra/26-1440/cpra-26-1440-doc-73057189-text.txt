borrowing of $35M General Obligation Bonds for Measure O – Affordable Housing and two, for $11M of Lease
Revenue Notes for the Fire Administration and Training Project. Per the City’s Debt and Disclosure Policy, The
Director of Finance constituted a Disclosure Working Group which consisted of The Director of Finance, Treasury
Manager and Acting Accounting Manager. The Disclosure Working Group have reviewed and approved all the
preliminary Official Statements before they were posted and certifies that all information in those documents are
factually correct to the best of our knowledge. Regards-Henry
