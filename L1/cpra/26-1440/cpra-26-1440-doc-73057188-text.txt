Dear Mayor and City Council-On June 30, 2026, the City Council will be approving two
Bond issuances. (1) The borrowing of $20M General Obligation Bonds for Measure T1 –
Infrastructure and Facilities (2) The borrowing of $85M for the Tax and Revenue
Anticipation Notes. Per the City’s Debt and Disclosure Policy, The Director of Finance
constituted a Disclosure Working Group which consisted of The Director of Finance,
 Acting Deputy Director of Finance and Acting Accounting Manager. The Disclosure
Working Group have reviewed and approved all the Preliminary Official Statements
before they were posted and certifies that all information in those documents are
factually correct to the best of our knowledge. Regards-Henry
