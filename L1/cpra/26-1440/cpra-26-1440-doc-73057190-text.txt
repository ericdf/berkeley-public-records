Good evening Mayor Ishii and all Councilmembers (bcc),
I’ve discussed this timeline with each of you but want you to have a copy you can refer back to when you
want. I’ve left it titled draft because it may still change some as we move forward, but it will be close to
what is outlined.

There are many significant dates on this calendar, most close at hand is 4/10 when employees whose
positions are recommended for elimination will be informed of that by department directors. All
employees in impacted classifications will also receive a notice informing them that they may be
impacted and union leadership will also receive this information. Employee names will not be on the
notices received by unions or all members of impacted classifications; there will be position numbers so
staff can identify themselves.

I plan to send the attached timeline to the organization on Tuesday in the weekly update, so I expect
that there will be noticeable upset and possibly media attention. From Tuesday through the end of June
budget adoption there will be many challenges to the organization and things will be difficult. To the
extent we stay focused, communicate well and make hard decisions with compassion we will all be
better off. I am available to any of you if you want to talk / review materials.

Thank you,
Paul
