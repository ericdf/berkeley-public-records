Berkeley Streets Underfunded
 City Council Actions
    June 30, 2022: City Council increased baseline funding for

     Fund Contribution for Street Maintenance to Prevent

    Transportation and Infrastructure Commission currently
     reviewing draft plan
    City Council adopts 5 Year Paving Plan in fall

Rocky Road: Berkeley Streets at Risk &
Significantly Underfunded
   Finding 2.1-2.3: The Streets Rehabilitation and Repair Policy is out-of-
    date and Public Works is not following it.
      Recommendation 2.1:    Update the Street Rehabilitation and Repair
       Policy annually and define who is responsible for ensuring the Policy is
       updated, as stated in the Policy.
      Recommendation 2.2:     When updating the Street Rehabilitation and
       Repair Policy, incorporate equity to align with Vision 2050 and clearly
       define how it will be applied to the street maintenance and
       rehabilitation planning process.
      Recommendation 2.3:     Define goals and performance measures to
       guide the Policy, align with other plans and policies relevant to street
       paving (Complete Streets, Vision 2050, etc.). Regularly report to
       Council on performance measures.
