CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM OVERVIEW
Five-Year Capital Improvement Program (CIP)
• Developed as part of the City’s biennial budget process
• Identifies anticipated capital projects and projected funding
• Guides long-term infrastructure planning and budget decisions

Year 1-2: Capital Budget
• Represents new funding appropriations for that fiscal year’s projects
• Adopted with the annual operating budget
• Functions as the most actionable portion of the CIP

Years 3–5: Planning
• Project lists and funding are for planning purposes
• Serves as a planning, budgetary, and management tool that is re-evaluated and updated regularly
to reflect evolving community needs, Council priorities, and fiscal conditions.

                     aging infrastructure.
