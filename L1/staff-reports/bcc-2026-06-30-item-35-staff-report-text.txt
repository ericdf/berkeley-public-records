RECOMMENDATION
Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager to amend Contract No. 32300127 with
AE3 Partners, Inc. for Architectural Services for the African American Holistic Resource
Center project, increasing the amount by $700,000 for an amended total amount not to
exceed $1,600,000 and extend the contract term from December 31, 2027 to June 30,
2029.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
Funding for this contract amendment is available in the FY 2026 budget in the Measure
T1 Fund (Fund 511). No other funding is required, and no other projects will be delayed
due to this expenditure.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The contract with AE3 Partners, Inc. for the African American Holistic Resource Center
(AAHRC) project was approved on July 26, 2022, for an amount not to exceed
$900,000, based on renovation of an existing facility of 4,000 sf. In 2023, based on
engineering assessments of the building and site (structural and geotechnical) which
found significant structural problems, City staff recommended that this building be
replaced, and City Council authorized the replacement of the building, leaving only the
size of the building to be determined. On June 13, 2023 City Council authorized City
staff to move forward with the design of a 6,000 sf facility.

The project completed conceptual design in 2024 and received Zoning approval and a
Use Permit in December 2025. During this time, several industry-wide changes directly
affected project costs and consultant availability. The architecture, engineering, and
specialty consulting markets have experienced significant inflationary cost escalation
since 2022. Several originally contracted subconsultants have also merged, closed or
discontinued their services, requiring AE3 Partners to procure replacement firms at
higher rates for service. The current not-to-exceed contract amount is projected to be
depleted by August 2026.

BACKGROUND
On December 15, 2020, the Berkeley City Council approved $7 million in revenue from
Measure T1 for the creation of an African American Holistic Resource Center in South
Berkeley.

A Request for Qualifications (RFQ) was issued on April 14, 2022, seeking qualified firms
or individuals to provide architectural services for the AAHRC. On May 17, 2022, the
City received four proposals from professional consultant firms. On June 3, a panel of 1
City staff, 1 consultant project manager, and 3 AAHRC Steering Committee members
reviewed, evaluated, and ranked the 4 proposals according to the criteria in the RFQ.
This panel referred the two highest-ranking proposals to the City Manager, who
received presentations from both firms. After these presentations, AE3 Partners, Inc.
was identified as the highest-rated proposal. On July 26, 2022, the City Council of the
City of Berkeley authorized the City Manager to approve a contract with AE3 Partners,
Inc. in an amount not to exceed $900,000.

On June 13, 2023, the Berkeley City Council approved an additional $6.85 million in
Measure T1 funds for the 6,000 sf facility. On July 14, 2025, the City Manager
authorized the extension of the contract from July 31, 2025 to December 31, 2027.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY
There are no anticipated negative environmental effects of this action. The execution of
this contract will help ensure the successful completion of the AAHRC project that will
incorporate the sourcing of sustainable and renewable resources used in construction
projects, as well as compliance with all regulatory agency requirements. The new
building will incorporate modern energy and water efficient design and technology,
promote environmental sustainability, and meets the Strategic Plan Goal to provide
state-of-the-art, well-maintained infrastructure, amenities, and facilities.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
The recommended contract amendment is necessary to ensure the African American
Holistic Resource Center project can advance through final design and into the building
permit review phase and construction. The project has already secured its Use Permit
and is completing Design Development, with final design anticipated in Fall 2026. To
