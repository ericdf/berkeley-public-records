II.   Background
      On October 5, 2023, the Landmarks Preservation Commission approved a Structural
      Alteration Permit #LMSAP2022-0011 to demolish the California Theater building while
      retaining and rehabilitating the primary façade, and to incorporate the façade into the
      construction of a new 18-story, mixed-use building featuring an approximately 24,000-
      sq. ft. performance venue and 211 dwelling units in the Downtown. On June 4, 2024,
      City Council upheld the Zoning Adjustments Board decision to approve the associated
      Use Permit for the project (#ZP2022-0144) after the decision had been appealed.

      On September 16, 2024, the applicant submitted a Use Permit Modification application
      (#ZP2024-0131) to modify the previously approved development project by increasing
      the permitted building height from 18 to 23 stories. The applicant submitted this requisite
      Structural Alteration Permit (SAP) application for the Use Permit Modification proposal
      on February 10, 2025. The SAP consideration is prescribed by the Landmarks
      Preservation Ordinance, Berkeley Municipal Code (BMC) Section 3.24.200, and serves
      as Design Review in accordance with the Zoning Ordinance, BMC Section 23.406.070.

      Both the Use Permit and SAP Modification applications are under review at this time.

      Pursuant to BMC Section 3.24.220 for Permit Application – data and public hearing
      required, the Commission must open a hearing on an alteration proposal within 70 days
      of receiving an application. For this reason, Commission is advised to open the hearing
      on this matter tonight and then continue the matter to allow time for the applicant to
      provide additional materials. In accordance with BMC Section 3.24.230.A for Public
      Hearing Notice Requirement, staff mailed and posted 10-day advance notices for
      tonight’s meeting on (or before) February 24, 2025.

III. Rationale for Recommendation
      The Commission must hold a public hearing within 70 days of the filing of an SAP
      application in accordance with BMC Section 3.24.220. Therefore, staff recommends that
      the Commission open the hearing on this matter to satisfy the regulatory requirement,
      take testimony from those members of the public who attend the hearing, and then
      continue the hearing and allow time to complete application review and to present a
      recommendation for action.

IV. Recommendation
      Staff recommends that the Commission open the public hearing on this matter in
      accordance with BMC Section 3.24.220, and then continue consideration of this request
      to a date to be determined.
