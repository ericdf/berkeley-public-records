Shaw Integrated and Turf Solutions, Inc. was selected through the California Multiple
Award Schedule (CMAS) bid procedure. Through the CMAS process, the City leverages
the State of California pre-qualified vendor pricing, enabling cost savings and faster
procurement.

The services provided will support the Strategic Plan goals of creating a resilient, safe,
connected and prepared city as well as providing state-of-the-art, well-maintained
facilities.

BACKGROUND
The broadloom carpets in the Civic Center Building are over twenty years old and need
replacement. with visiable failures, such as crushed padding, seam separation, and
heavy staining. Based on current schedules and availability, staff anticipate performing
the replacement in Fall 2025.

The City developed technical specifications for carpet tiles in 2015, which have been
since used in multiple projects, including the Public Safety Building (2017) and the Civic
Center (2180 Milvia Street) in 2020 and 2022. The carpet tiles have proven durable,
low-maintenance, and cost effective.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
The carpet tiles meet the City’s criteria sustainability requirements, including being PVC
free and 100% recyclable, constentent with the Berkeley General Plan, Climate Action
Plan, and CalGreen.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
Contracted services are required for this project as the City does not have the in-house
expertise to complete this specialized work. Shaw Integrated and Turf Solutions, Inc.
provided a cost-effective price, complying with the City’s carpet specifications.

ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS CONSIDERED
If a new RFP were to be managed by Staff to acquire a vendor, rather than using
CMAS, the process would be longer and potentially be impacted by increased costs for
materials and labor. CMAS is the most time and cost-effective action.
