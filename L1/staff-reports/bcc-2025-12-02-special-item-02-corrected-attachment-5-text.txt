“Good of the City” Analysis:
The analysis below must demonstrate how accepting this supplement/revision is for the “good of
the City” and outweighs the lack of time for citizen review or evaluation by the Council.
 This supplemental corrects an administrative error in Attachment 5, pp. 43-45. The
 total project amount for Parks, Recreation, and Waterfront should read
 $107,600,000.00. The corrected amount is shown in red and highlighted in the
 revised attachment.


 “Good of the City” Analysis
 Accepting this supplement is for the good of the City because it ensures accurate
 financial information for Council decision-making. The correction is administrative and
 does not change policy direction, and the benefit of providing accurate data outweighs
 the limited time for additional review.
