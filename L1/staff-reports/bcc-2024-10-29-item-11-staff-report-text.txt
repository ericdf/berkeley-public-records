                                                                CONSENT CALENDAR
                                                                 October 29, 2024
To:              Honorable Mayor and Members of the City Council
From:            Paul Buddenhagen, City Manager
Submitted by: Scott Ferris, Director, Parks Recreation and Waterfront
Subject:         Contract: American Debris Box Services, Inc. for Portable Toilet Service

RECOMMENDATION
Adopt a Resolution authorizing the City Manager or his designee to execute a contract
and any amendments with American Debris Box Services, Inc. for portable toilet service
in an amount not to exceed $360,000 for a period of two years, with an option to extend
up to three additional 12-month periods for a total amount not to exceed $900,000.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
Funding for this contract is included in the FY 2025 and FY 2026 Parks Tax Fund
(Fund 138) and General Fund (Fund 011) for ongoing rental and service of portable
toilets in City parks, special events and emergency orders. Funding is subject to
appropriation in future fiscal years in the Parks Tax Fund and General Fund.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The current contract for portable toilet service with United Site Services of California,
Inc. ends on December 31, 2024. The City issued a Request for Proposal on August 8,
2024 for portable toilet service (Spec No. 24-11670-C) and received six proposals on
August 27, 2024. A review panel comprised of City staff identified American Debris Box
Services, Inc. (doing business as American Portables) as the most responsive proposal
to the requirements specified in the RFP. Staff therefore recommends approval of a
contract with American Debris Box Services, Inc., to provide portable toilet service to
the City for a two-year period, with options to extend up to three additional years.

BACKGROUND
Since 2014, the Parks, Recreation and Waterfront Department has contracted with
United Site Services of California, Inc. for portable toilet service at City parks, special
events, and other needed City locations. Units are located in heavily used parks that do
not have appropriate alternative restroom facilities or the existing restrooms do not meet
American with Disabilities Act requirements. Additional units are placed as needed for

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
American Debris Box Services, Inc. uses several methods to reduce the impact of its
operations on the environment, and therefore comply with the City’s environmental
sustainability policies. To reduce fuel usage and carbon emissions in all service
vehicles, American Debris Box Services, Inc. has installed automatic idle limiters in all
vehicles. To eliminate the use of harmful chemicals, American Debris Box Services, Inc.
uses the following “green” materials in their portable toilets: formalehyde-free toilet
deodorizers; citrus-based non-caustic, non-flammable cleaning solutions; and refillable
hand pump dispensers instead of aerosol cans. All materials are purchased in bulk to
reduce package waste. The toilet paper and paper towels are made from recycled
paper.
