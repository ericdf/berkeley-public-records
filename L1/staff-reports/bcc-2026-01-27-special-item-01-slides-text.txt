              Reweighted Range Voting (RRV)
How it works:
• Each Councilmember rates every referral on a scale of 0-5 (0 being the least
  support and five being the most support) using the ServiceNow RRV Portal.
• There is no limit to repeat scores (i.e. giving every referral a 5)
• The scores are tallied, and the referral with the highest total score becomes
  1st priority.
• Then, for the remaining referrals, the scores are reweighted based on how
  much influence each Counvilmember has had up to that point. This
  guarantees equal influence over the long run.

            How the Current Process Works
• Council adopts the referral
• Staff categorizes the referral
   • Long Term (more than 90 days to respond)
   • Short Term (less than 90 days to respond)
   • Urgent (health and safety, deadline-based funding)
• Staff adds Long Term to the list for RRV process
• Staff starts work on Short Term and Urgent referrals
• Council rates the Long Term list annually using RRV
• Staff addresses the referrals generally in the order of Council priority
• Completed Referrals reported back though Council Agenda or Off-Agenda Memo
