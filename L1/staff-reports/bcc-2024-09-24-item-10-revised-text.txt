RECOMMENDATION
In order to ensure that voters are fully informed and to inform future discussions of
potential expenditures, it is recommended that the Berkeley City Council adopt the
following language to clarify (but not change) the intent of Measure Y:

        The intent of Measure Y is to generate additional funds by increasing the Parks
        Tax for park and landscape maintenance in the Waterfront including Cesar
        Chavez (Berkeley’s largest park), improvements to Berkeley’s urban forest and
        street tree plantings, and essential park and landscaping related capital projects.

FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS
None. Staff time to record resolutionclarification.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
At a special meeting on July 30, 2024, the Berkeley City Council unanimously
approved 1 Item 6c to 2:

        1. Adopt a Resolution placing the attached measure to increase the parks, trees
        and landscaping maintenance tax on the ballot at the November 5, 2024 General
        Municipal Election.

        2. Designate, by motion, specific members of the Council to file ballot measure
        arguments on this measure as provided for in Elections Code Section 9282.

BACKGROUND
In an item from the December 12, 2023 Council Meeting 3, the Parks, Recreation and
Waterfront Commission recommended that the City Council include in upcoming
community survey for possible 2024 ballot measures, a measure to increase the Parks
Tax by 4ct/ft2 or ~20%, and appropriate funding for the survey.

This recommendation was based on action at the regular Parks, Recreation and
Waterfront Commission meeting on November 8, 2023. At that meeting, the
Commission approved a motion to communicate a recommendation to Council to
include an increase in the Parks Tax by 4cts/SF or ~20% in polling for possible
November 2024 ballot measures. The estimated $3.5M annually, would be divided
between: major maintenance/capital projects, Urban Forest restoration, and
consolidation of three Waterfront Parks into City Park system.

The vote at the meeting was:

M/S/C (Avellar/Kawczynska/U). Ayes: Abshez; Avellar; Cox; Diehm; Kawczynska; Lee;
Ranuzzi; Wozniak; Noes: None; Absent: Hurtado; Leave of Absence: None.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
None. The resolution clarification makes no change to the originally intended uses of
potential funds; it merely formalizes and makes explicit the intent verbally discussed at
previous Council meetings.
