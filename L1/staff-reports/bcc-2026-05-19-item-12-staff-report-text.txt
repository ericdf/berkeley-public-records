This project was advertised for bids on November 3, 2025, under Specification No. 25-
11684-C. Bids were opened on December 11, 2025. The City Received three (3) bids in
the amounts ranging from $1,090,811 to $1,975,000. The lowest responsive and

recommends that a contract for the Project be awarded to Bay Construction.

The services provided through the contract will support the Strategic Plan goals of
creating a resilient, safe, connected, and prepared city and providing state-of-the-art,
well-maintained infrastructure, amenities, and facilities.

Effective January 1, 2026, local agencies are required to satisfy noticing requirements
for certain contracts for services that meet the criteria outlined in Government Code
Section 3504.1 as amended by AB 339. This project does not meet the criteria in
Government Code Section 3504.1 and is exempt from the noticing requirement.

BACKGROUND
The Public Safety Building was constructed in 2000. In 2022, the Berkeley Police
Department requested support from Public Works to fund and manage the jail
automation upgrade project. The existing control panel and security systems have
exceeded their expected service life, and replacement parts are increasingly difficult to
obtain. This upgrade project is intended to address critical life-safety concerns.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
There are no identified environmental or climate impacts associated with the project.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
The existing control panel and security systems have exceeded their expected service
life, and replacement parts are increasingly difficult to obtain. If the control panel stops
functioning, all jail cells and doors would need to be opened manually using hard keys.
This creates a life-safety concern for both staff and occupants of the facility.

ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS CONSIDERED
No alternatives were considered, since the system is outdated and requires
replacement.

                             RESOLUTION NO.          -N.S.

     CONTRACT: BAY CONSTRUCTION FOR THE PUBLIC SAFETY BUILDING
                   AUTOMATION UPGRADE PROJECT

WHEREAS, the Public Safety Building Jail requires a new security and control system to
upgrade the detention area; and

WHEREAS, an invitation for bids (Plans and Specifications No. 25-11684-C) was duly
advertised, on November 3, 2025; and

WHEREAS, Bay Construction was determined to be the lowest responsive and
responsible bidder with a bid in the amount of $1,090,811; and

WHEREAS, funds are available in the FY 2026 budget in the Capital Improvements Fund;
and

WHEREAS, this contract is exempt from the noticing requirements set forth in
Government Code Section 3504.1.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Council of the City of Berkeley authorizes
the City Manager to execute a contract and any amendments, extensions, or change
orders, until completion of the project in accordance with the approved plans and
specifications with Bay Construction for the Public Safety Building Jail Automation
Upgrade project at 2100 Martin Luther King Jr. Way, in an amount not to exceed
$1,308,973.00, which includes a 20% contingency for unforeseen conditions. A record
copy of the agreement and any amendments will be on file in the Office of the City Clerk.
