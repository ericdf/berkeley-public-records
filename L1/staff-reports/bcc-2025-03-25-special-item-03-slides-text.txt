              Reweighted Range Voting (RRV)
How it works:
• Each Councilmember rates every referral on a scale of 0-5 (0 being the least
  support and five being the most support) using the ServiceNow RRV Portal.
• There is no limit to repeat scores (i.e. giving every referral a 5)
• The scores are tallied, and the referral with the highest total score becomes
  1st priority.
• Then, for the remaining referrals, the scores are reweighted based on how
  much influence each CM has had up to that point. This guarantees equal
  influence over the long run.
