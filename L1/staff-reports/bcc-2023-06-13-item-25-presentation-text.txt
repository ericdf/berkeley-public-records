        BUDGET AND FINANCE COMMITTEE

May 4, 2023 Committee Recommendation:
• Fully Fund 6000 Square foot AAHRC and $9.062M T1 Gap by doing the
  following:
  • Use $4,050,000 from the Workers Compensation Fund
  • Use $2,800,000 of $6,750,000 (T1 P2 Streets) allocated for the Hopkins Bike/Pedestrian
    project*
  • Use $500,000 (T1 P2) from the North Berkeley Senior Center (NBSC) Solar Battery
    Storage project
  • Use $643,899 of $1,293,889 from the Street/ Intersection Security Camera Project
    (General Fund)
  • Use $666,101 of $1,300,000 (T1P2) from Fire Station 6 project. This project becomes
    Planning and Design only.
