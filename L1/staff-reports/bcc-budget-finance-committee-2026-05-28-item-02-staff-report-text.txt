RECOMMENDATION
Request that the Budget & Finance Policy Committee receive, review, and discuss the
City Manager’s Proposed FY 2027 - FY 2031 Capital Improvement Program.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
The Proposed Capital Improvement Program (CIP) presents planned citywide capital
projects for Fiscal Years 2027–2031. It includes the Capital Budget for FY 2027 and FY
2028, covering expenditures in the Capital Improvement Program Fund, Enterprise
Funds, Internal Service Funds, and Special Revenue Funds. Planned projects and
projected funding sources for FY 2029–2031 are also included.

Fiscal impacts may change based on Committee input and Council direction and will be
finalized with budget adoption on June 23, 2026.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The Proposed Five‑Year CIP provides a comprehensive plan to address the City’s
infrastructure needs. It identifies specific capital projects and associated funding
sources for the FY 2027 and FY 2028 Biennial Budget, as well as projected funding and
planned projects for FY 2029–2031. The CIP also outlines the City’s significant
unfunded capital needs.

CONCLUSION
The Budget and Finance Policy Committee and the City Council will continue
deliberations on the Proposed FY 2027- FY 2031 Capital Improvement Program before
its final adoption on June 23, 2026.

The sequence of meetings below provides for public input, Committee input and Council
direction, and refinement of the Proposed FY 2027 – FY 2031 CIP, prior to adoption.

    •   May 28, 2026 - Budget and Finance Policy Committee: Proposed 5-Year CIP

    •   June 9, 2026 – City Council: Public Hearing No. 1 on Proposed 5-Year CIP

    •   June 11, 2026 – Budget and Finance Policy Committee: Mayor’s Budget
        Recommendation and Community Agencies

    •   June 16, 2026 – City Council: Potential Discussion on Proposed Biennial Budget
        and 5-Year CIP

    •   June 23, 2026 – City Council: Public Hearing No. 2 and Adoption of Proposed
        Biennial Budget and 5-Year CIP
