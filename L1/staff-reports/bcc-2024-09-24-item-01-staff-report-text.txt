Subject:         Contract: B-Bros Construction Inc. for Temporary Stabilization of Stair Tower
                   Windows at 2180 Milvia Street

RECOMMENDATION
Adopt a Resolution approving the City Manager’s emergency authorization of a contract
with B-Bros Construction Inc. to make necessary repairs to the windows in the
secondary egress stairwells in 2180 Milvia Street in an amount not to exceed $400,000.

NEGATIVE EFFECT IF ACTION IS DELAYED UNTIL AFTER COUNCIL RECESS
On or about July 11, 2024, Public Works staff was alerted to potential hazards caused
by windows in the secondary egress stairwells in 2180 Milvia Street. Glass from the
windows could fall from building, with people and property at risk of being hit by glass.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
Funding is available out of the baseline FY 25 Facilities CIP Deferred
Maintenance/Emergency Projects budget from the Capital Improvement Fund 501. Not
making the windows and the area below and around them safe could make the City of
Berkeley liable for any death, injury, damage or other harm caused by falling glass.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
On or about July 11, 2024, Public Works staff was alerted to potential hazards caused
by windows in the secondary egress stairwells in 2180 Milvia Street. After an inspection
with Public Works and Build Safety Staff on Thursday morning, July 11, City staff
developed the following action plan to help mitigate any potential safety issues.

         Site: 2180 Milvia Street, secondary egress stairwell towers near the parking lot
         Hazard: Glass from windows could fall outside the building
         Risk: Passersby outside the building could be at risk of falling glass; City Staff
          can still safely work inside the building, and members of the public can still go
          inside; this level of risk does not require the shutdown of 2180 Milvia Street

Public Works has contract B-Bros Construction Inc. to furnish labor and materials in
accordance with the City’s plan and specifications for necessary repairs to the windows
in the secondary egress stairway at 2180 Milvia Street.

Public Works staff will continue to provide updates as the work makes progress.

BACKGROUND
In July, Public Works worked through emergency design procedures with the Planning
Department to install fencing and safety nets to secure and mitigate the hazard. This
allowed staff to continue their engineering investigation and determine the appropriate
steps to repair this landmark building.

Immediate course of action in July –

    1. Public Works and other City Staff:
         a. Installed temporary safety barriers, caution tape and channelizers to keep
              public and employees clear of the zones; and,
         b. Accelerated emergency mitigation design work and obtain mitigation plan
              permit approval from Planning.
    2. Public Works contracted to temporarily install:
         a. Scaffolding for the mitigation while the design is being finalized;
         b. Fencing to make the area under the windows safe; and,
         c. Netting to contain glass in the event that it does fall, preventing it from
              falling to the ground and possibly injuring people and causing damage.

Public Communication – City Staff and visitors to the 2180 Milvia Street building have
been instructed to use the secondary stairwell only as an emergency egress. Staff put
up:

               Notices that state the secondary stairwell is for emergency egress only;
                and,
               Signage to guide people in the event of an emergency evacuation.

This hazard does not affect the primary stairwell inside the building (past lobby, near the
rear elevators).

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
There are no identifiable environmental effects, climate impacts, or sustainability
opportunities associated with the subject of this report.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
The risk of windows falling off of 2180 Milvia Street created an extremely dangerous
condition that the City must urgently address.
