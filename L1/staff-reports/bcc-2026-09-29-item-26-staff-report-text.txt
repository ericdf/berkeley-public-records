RECOMMENDATION
Refer to the City Manager to develop an updated cross-departmental Program Plan and Strategic
Asset Management Plan as set forth in the Realize Vision 2050 Plan to address the City’s long-
term infrastructure needs and maintenance schedule, and provide semi-annual progress reports to
City Council.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The City of Berkeley has approximately $1.5 billion in unfunded infrastructure needs. Currently,
the City is taking steps to address this need, but to effectively accomplish this goal, it is
imperative that the City continue to amplify its work to understand the landscape of the need and
have criteria for setting priorities. To accomplish this, the City needs an updated Program Plan
and Strategic Asset Management Plan. A Program Plan is a high-level strategic document that
outlines the City’s long-term organizational goals and coordinates projects across the appropriate
departments. This document will outline all the infrastructure projects Citywide that need to be
completed, attached with a rough cost analysis of each project and a prioritization plan informed
by urgency and the Realize Vision 2050 plan. The Asset Management Plan is a roadmap for
managing the City’s physical assets, including infrastructure, equipment and facilities over the
course of their life cycles. This will work in tandem with the Program Plan to help the
Departments keep track of maintenance plans for the City’s assets.

To promote transparency on how the City is progressing and addressing its infrastructure needs,
City staff should institute a transparency hub dashboard to publicly track its infrastructure
projects. This dashboard could be akin to the Transportation Studio: Capital Improvement

The City Manager will provide semi-annual (every six months) progress reports on the Program
Plan and the Strategic Asset Management Plan to the City Council.

BACKGROUND
The City of Berkeley maintains over 300 miles of sidewalks, 54 parks, 95 buildings, as well as
municipal drains, streets and much more.2 In 2020, the City of Berkeley put forth the Vision
2050 plan, which is “a long-term infrastructure plan to create a city that is resilient and
sustainable for future generations.”3 Following the November 16, 2021 Vision 2050 Progress
Report4, on May 10, 2022, the Berkeley City Council approved Vision 2050: Strategic Asset
Management Plan and Asset Management Policy.5 Because infrastructure is one of Mayor
Adena Ishii’s priority areas, she reconvened the Vision 2050 Task Force in 2025 to update the
plan, with consideration to the significant changes that have occurred between 2020 and 2025.6
This new plan, called Realize Vision 2050, iterates the need to implement both an Infrastructure
Program Plan, as well as an Asset Management Plan. Both plans are integral to project
prioritization and creating a comprehensive landscape of City infrastructure needs. Both Vision
2050 and Realize Vision 2050 should be foundational in building out the plans.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY
There are no environmental impacts generated by this referral.

FISCAL IMPACTS
The fiscal impact to update the Program Plan and the Strategic Asset Management Plan is
estimated at approximately $200,000, which includes staff and consultant time.
