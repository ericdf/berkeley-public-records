RECOMMENDATION
Adopt a resolution authorizing the City Manager to execute an amendment to Contract
No. 32500050 with The Dutra Group for the Berkeley Marina Docks D&E Replacement
Project, increasing the amount by $300,000 for an amended total amount not to exceed
$8,893,200.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
This contract amendment will add an additional $300,000 to the existing construction
contract with The Dutra Group to cover the change orders needed to complete the
project. Funding in the amount of $176,000 from the Marina Fund (608) will be used for
the work at the D &E Dock Replacement Project and $124,000 from the PRW CIP
General Fund (011) will be used for the urgent piling repair work at the L & M Docks.
FY 27 funding for this contract amendment will be recommended for appropriation to
carryover unspent FY 26 capital funds through the first amendment to the
appropriations ordinance in the General Fund (Fund 011) and Marina Fund (Fund 608).
No other funding is required, and no other projects will be delayed due to this
expenditure.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
The Berkeley Marina Docks D&E Replacement project includes a new water line for a
dedicated fire water system and a new transformer to accommodate the increase in
power provided at the slips. These upgraded utilities require upstream infrastructure
improvements performed by the utility companies. Due to delays associated with
EBMUD and PG&E permitting and scheduling, the project has incurred additional costs.

During the permitting phase, updated codes required fire line pipe upsizing, additional
testing, and additional sidewalk demolition and repaving. During the scheduling phase,
PG&E has been significantly delayed in installing their transformer. Because the utilities

BACKGROUND
The Berkeley Marina Docks D & E Replacement Project was duly advertised for
competitive bids on January 31, 2024, and bids were opened on March 14, 2024. Four
(4) bids were received ranging from a low base bid of $7,812,000 to a high base bid of
$8,765,000. Staff determined that The Dutra Group of San Rafael, CA was the lowest
responsive and responsible bidder and executed contract number 32500050.

The floating dock system at Docks D and E was constructed in the early 1970s and is
well past the original expected useful life of 15 years. About 40 to 50 percent of its slips
have degraded to the point of being unusable. The purpose of this project is to replace
the entire floating dock system with an improved system that consists of: ADA slips and
gangway, longer lasting concrete docks, chemically-inert concrete piles, code compliant
utilities such as electrical, potable water and fire suppression systems, along with
landside improvements as such a new security gate structure and shoreline slope
protection.

The piers that serve the gangways to Docks L and M are supported by two timber
creosote piles each. During a recent inspection, all four timber creosote piles were
found to be significantly damaged. Damage consists of rotten wood and voids near the
waterline due to tidal action and marine boring organisms. Although the percent of voids
in the piles is very high it will be possible to repair the piles in place versus requiring full
replacement of the piles. Delaying this work could cause pile failure requiring closure of
the pier.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
The construction contract includes requirements to comply with the City’s
Environmentally Preferable Purchasing Policy. The work will also comply with the
conditions contained in the Bay Conservation Development Commission (BCDC)
Permit, US Army Corps of Engineers Permit, San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality
Control Board Permit and City of Berkeley Building Permit.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
This project is part of the City’s ongoing effort to update and maintain the deteriorated
infrastructure at the Berkeley Marina. The project will help the City advance Strategic
Plan Long-term goal number 1 to provide state-of-the-art, well-maintained infrastructure,
amenities, and facilities.

                             RESOLUTION NO. ##,###-N.S.

     CONTRACT NO. 32500050 AMENDMENT: THE DUTRA GROUP FOR THE
         BERKELEY MARINA DOCKS D&E REPLACEMENT PROJECT

WHEREAS, the existing floating dock system at Docks D & E at the Berkeley Marina
was originally constructed in the 1960s and has well past the original expected useful
life of 15 years, it is in need of replacement; and

WHEREAS, an invitation for bids was duly advertised on January 31, 2024, bids were
opened on March 14, 2024, and the City received four (4) bids; and

WHEREAS, the bid from THE DUTRA GROUP was the lowest responsive, responsible
bid of $7,812,000, and references for THE DUTRA GROUP were provided and checked
out satisfactorily; and

WHEREAS, the contract with THE DUTRA GROUP for the Berkeley Marina Docks D&E
Replacement Project was executed on May 7, 2024 for a total amount not to exceed
$8,593,200, including a construction contingency (Resolution No. 71,286-N.S.); and

WHEREAS, two contract amendments have been executed to date to address
unanticipated existing conditions and conflicts, resulting in a current total contract value
of $8,571,195.50; and

WHEREAS, unforeseen upstream utility infrastructure improvement conditions and
emergency pile repair work require additional funds to complete the work; and

WHEREAS, funding in the amount of $176,000 from the Marina Fund (608) will be used
for the work at the D &E Dock Replacement Project and $124,000 from the PRW CIP
General Fund (011) will be used for the urgent piling repair work at the L & M Docks. FY
27 funding for this contract amendment will be recommended for appropriation to
carryover unspent FY 26 capital funds through the first amendment to the appropriations
ordinance in the General Fund (Fund 011) and Marina Fund (Fund 608).

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of the City of Berkeley that the
City Manager is authorized to execute an amendment to Contract No. 32500050 with THE
DUTRA GROUP for the Berkeley Marina Docks D&E Replacement Project, increasing
the contract amount by $300,000 for a total amended amount not to exceed $8,893,200.
A record signature copy of any amendments to be on file in the Office of the City Clerk.
