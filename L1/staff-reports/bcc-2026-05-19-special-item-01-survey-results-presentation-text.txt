Project Evaluation Methodology & Rubric
                    Criteria                       Weight                      What It Evaluates
              Health, Life & Safety                  20%     Immediate risk reduction, public health, emergency response
     Infrastructure Condition, Accessibility &
                    Resilience
                                                     20%         Asset condition, ADA access, lifecycle performance

            Community Use & Equity                   20%          Public benefit, high-use facilities, equitable access
        Green Resiliency & Sustainability            20%         Direct climate, adaptation, decarbonization benefits
           Deliverability & Readiness                10%              Scope clarity, approvals, timing, feasibility
           External Funding Potential                10%                  Grants, matching funds, leverage

                  Scoring Approach                                                   Priority Bands
 Rated Low (1) / Medium (2) / High (3) across six criteria      High: 75–100 | Medium: 50–74 | Low: Below 50
      Weighted Score = (Rating ÷ 3) × Weight
