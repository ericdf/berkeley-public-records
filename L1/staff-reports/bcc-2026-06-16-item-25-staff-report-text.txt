Subject:         Contract No. 32500082 Amendment: Telegraph Property and Business

RECOMMENDATION
Adopt a resolution amending the current contract with the Telegraph Property and
Business Management Corporation, commonly known as the Telegraph Business
Improvement District, for the janitorial maintenance of the 24-hour, seven days a week,
public restroom increasing the not-to-exceed amount by $150,000 for a new not-to-
exceed amount of $300,000 and extend the contract through June 30, 2027.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
There are funds available in the proposed FY2027 Public Works Budget (Fund 011-
General Fund) for janitorial services, pending approval from Council.

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
Public Works has contracted with the Telegraph Property and Business Management
Corporation (commonly known as the Telegraph Business Improvement District (TBID))
for the janitorial services for the public restroom on Channing Way near the intersection
with Telegraph Avenue. They perform regular, daily cleaning work for this facility
throughout the year to ensure it is operational and sanitary for users. They also restock
bathroom toiletries and other supplies during these cleanings.

Amending the contract with the servicing of the public restroom is in alignment with the
City’s Strategic Plan, advancing our goal to provide state-of-the-art, well-maintained
infrastructure, amenities, and facilities.

Effective January 1, 2026, local agencies are required to satisfy noticing requirements
for certain contracts for services that meet the criteria outlined in Government Code
Section 3204.1 as amended by AB 339. This project does not meet the criteria in
Government Code Section 3504.1 and is exempt from the noticing requirement.

BACKGROUND
TBID was selected through a sole source agreement in January of 2023 in advance of
the completion of the restroom which was anticipated in April 2023. The original contract

TBID is currently best suited to oversee this work as they have Ambassadors that will

inspecting the restroom three times a day, seven days a week, and will have easy
access to their supplies within the Telegraph Channing Garage. Additionally,
coordinating with TBID was a recommendation of the Citywide Restroom Study as TBID
has Ambassadors that are readily able to report issues to the City.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
Providing ongoing janitorial service for the 24-hours a day, seven days a week, public
restroom will keep the restroom open and usable by the community. Public restrooms
may help reduce open defecation which impacts stormwater and public health. There
are no identifiable climate impacts.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
Amending the existing contract is the most efficient and effective use of City resources.

ALTERNATIVE ACTIONS CONSIDERED
Without extending the contract, the City will have to close this restroom as there are no
available City janitorial staff to perform the same work.

                            RESOLUTION NO. ##,###-N.S.

     CONTRACT NO. 32500082 AMENDMENT: TELEGRAPH PROPERTY AND
               BUSINESS MANAGEMENT CORPORATION

WHEREAS, Public Works has contracted with the Telegraph Property and Business
Management Corporation (commonly known as the Telegraph Business Improvement
District (TBID)) for the janitorial services for the public restroom on Channing Way near
the intersection with Telegraph Avenue; and

WHEREAS, on February 25, 2025, City Council authorized the City Manager to execute
a Contract Amendment to increase the Contract’s spending authority by $100,000 for a
new not to exceed amount of $150,000 and to extend the Contract through June 30,
2026; and

WHEREAS, funding is available for maintenance and janitorial services in the proposed
FY2027 budget pending Council approval; and

WHEREAS, the TBID is well positioned to manage the janitorial services and provide
additional support of notification of issues through their Ambassador Program; and

WHEREAS, the City requires the services of TBID to provide janitorial maintenance of
the public restroom on Channing Way at Telegraph Avenue.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of the City of Berkeley that
Amending the contract with the Telegraph Property and Business Management
Corporation, commonly known as the Telegraph Business Improvement District, for the
janitorial maintenance of the new 24/7 public restroom with a new not to exceed amount
of $300,000 and extend the contract through June 30, 2027.
