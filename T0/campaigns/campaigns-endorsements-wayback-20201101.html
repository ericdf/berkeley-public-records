Primary Election 2018 Endorsement Process

Hello Democratic Candidates!

If you are interested in being considered for endorsement by the Alameda County Democratic Central Committee, you are in the right place. In order to be considered, you need to do three things:

1. Fill out the below form or click here.
2. Pay the $50 fee.
3. Get two Ex-Officio or elected members of the Alameda County Democratic Central Committee to recommend you for consideration of endorsement.

All three items must be completed by 6:15pm on Wednesday, March 28th, 2018. We will endeavor to schedule you for a timeslot at our April 4th, 2018 meeting by Friday, March 30th.

To request an email contact list of voting ACDCC members, or to request consideration of a ballot measure at our April 4th meeting, please email us at endorse@acdems.org.

NOTE: The Alameda County Democratic Party only endorses Democrats and does not endorse more candidates than seats to be filled.
