    How do I know if I am a member?
    To see if your membership is current, login to the member portal. Your username is your email address. You can reset your password if you don't know it. If you have questions, send us an email (eastbaystonewalldems@gmail.com).

    Can I still vote if I am unable to attend?
    Yes, in fact all voting will happen through our website.  You will need your login information to gain access, and you must be a member in good standing, with membership dues paid at least seven days prior. Candidate questionnaires will also be available on the website.

    What is the threshold for endorsement?
    If any one candidate receives at least 60% of the vote, they will be considered endorsed by Stonewall and would be eligible for support from the club as appropriate in accordance with the bylaws.

    Who is eligible for endorsement?
    Per the club’s bylaws, to be eligible for endorsement, a candidate must be a registered Democrat and must submit a completed endorsement questionnaire by the deadline. Per Board policy, a questionnaire is required only when there is more than one candidate in the election who is potentially eligible for endorsement. Please note that the Board has determined that only candidates who are eligible for endorsement by the Alameda County Democratic Party in this election cycle will be considered for endorsement by Stonewall. 

    Who is eligible to vote in the endorsement process?
    Current Stonewall members in good standing will be given the opportunity to vote at the endorsement meeting. The voting will be taking place on our website platform Wild Apricot via the Member portal. Members will need login information to gain access. The link will be provided to members at the time of the endorsement meeting.

    Can people still join the club to participate in the endorsement process?
    The deadline to join was September 12th. A member in good standing must have paid yearly membership dues at least 7 days prior to the endorsement meeting. Those wishing to join the club may sign up for membership on our website to be eligible to vote in future meetings.  If you believe you are already a member, you can verify your membership by logging in to the member portal here. Your email is your username. You can reset your password if you don't know it. If you have any questions as to whether you are an eligible voting member, please email, eastbaystonewalldems@gmail.com.
