Berkeley Democratic Club 2024  Endorsed Primary Candidates 

The BDC Board of Directors is excited to announce the results of our endorsement meeting held on Thursday, January 11, 2024, for the primary election in March 2024. Approximately Ninety 90 members participated and heard from an excellent field of Democrats running to represent us at the state and local levels. To be endorsed, a candidate must have received 60% of the votes cast in their race. Thank you to all the members who voted and the candidates who participated!

U.S. Congress D12: 

Lateefah Simon

State Senate D7: 

Jesse Arreguin

State Assembly D14: 

Buffy Wicks

Alameda County Supervisor District 5: 

John Bauters

Alameda County Superior Court, Seat 12:

Michael Johnson

Alameda County Democratic Central Committee AD14: 

Andy Kelley, Elizabeth Echols, Terry Taplin

Berkeley City Council Special Election District 7:

Cecelia Lunaparra

Ballot Measure BESP:

Yes on Measure H
