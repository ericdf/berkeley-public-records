Racial & Criminal Justice Committee meeting on Nov 8th

October 19, 2018 by The Wellstone Club

The new Racial & Criminal Justice Committee invites everyone to our next meeting on Thursday, November 8th at 7:00 p.m. at 1256 Monterey Ave., Berkeley (half a block north of Monterey Market). We are meeting jointly with members of Indivisible Berkeley, Democratic Socialists of America, and the Ella Baker Center for Human Rights who also are organizing on racial and criminal justice issues. We’ll celebrate and/or commiserate briefly about the election results and then focus on how to move forward on the local and state levels. For more info, contact Rivka Polatnick (rivkapol@hotmail.com).
